The Bodenschneidhaus belongs to the DAV's Bodenschneid section and stands on
the Rettenböckalm, about 300 m below and east of the Bodenschneid (1668 m).
The Alpenclub München bought a small alm here in 1906 and finished the wooden
main house in 1916. It was raised in the 1960s, renovated in 2011, and in 2024
received the DAV environmental seal for its water supply and waste disposal.

The quickest ways up come from Neuhaus am Schliersee through the Dürnbachtal
or from Schliersee station, each about 1.5 to 2 hours on forest roads. From
the Spitzingsattel it is about 1.5 hours over the Obere Firstalm and the
Bodenschneid Sommerweg around the Krettenburg, a rocky path for experienced
hikers that is closed in winter for avalanche danger; the winter route goes
over the Freudenreichsattel instead. From the hut, the Bodenschneid is an
hour away and the Brecherspitz (1683 m) about an hour and a half.

- **Open:** daily 9:00 to 22:00, roughly May to February.
- **Beds:** 48, in small rooms and two 8-bed dormitories; book through the DAV
  reservation portal. Bring a hut sleeping bag or rent bedding on site.
- **Food:** regional dishes, Weißwurst and pretzels on weekends.
- **Website:** [bodenschneidhaus.de](https://bodenschneidhaus.de/)
