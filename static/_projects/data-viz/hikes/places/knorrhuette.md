The Knorrhütte belongs to the DAV's Munich section and sits on a rock spur
below the Zugspitzplatt (2588 m), about two hours under the summit of
Zugspitze (2962 m). It was first built in 1855 as the first shelter built in
the Zugspitze area.

It is the usual overnight stop on the long approaches to Germany's highest
peak: up the Reintal from Garmisch-Partenkirchen, or from Ehrwald over the
Gatterl (2024 m). From the hut the trail climbs onto the Platt, past
Sonnalpin, to the cable car stations and the summit.

- **Open:** roughly late May to early October, staffed in season.
- **Beds:** about 110, in rooms and dormitories; book ahead in summer.
- **Water:** the only reliable source between the Gatterl and Sonnalpin.
- **Getting down:** the Reintal path to Garmisch takes most of a day; the
  quicker way off the Platt is the Gletscherbahn and a cable car or the cog
  railway from Sonnalpin.
