The Firstalm is a large private alm west of the Spitzingsee, at the end of the
valley between the Brecherspitz (1683 m) and the Stümpfling (1506 m). Its name
comes from the roof-ridge (First) shape of the Bodenschneid to the west. Two
inns share the pasture: the Untere Firstalm on the valley floor by the ski
lifts, and the Obere Firstalm about 400 m to the north-east and 50 m higher.

From the Spitzingsattel bus stop a closed forest road climbs to the inn in
about 45 minutes; in winter the same road becomes a lit toboggan run, with
sledges for hire at the hut. The Obere Firstalm is a common stop on the way
to the Brecherspitz, the Bodenschneid and the Bodenschneidhaus. Right behind
it, the south face of the Krettenburg (1494 m) is a climbing crag with over
a hundred routes.

- **Open:** daily until 22:00, kitchen until 20:00; breakfast buffet 8:00 to
  10:00.
- **Rooms:** double, four-bed and family rooms and a holiday flat.
- **Access:** on foot only; cars need the innkeepers' permission.
