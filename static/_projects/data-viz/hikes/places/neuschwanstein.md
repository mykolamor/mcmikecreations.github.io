---
title: Schloss Neuschwanstein
description: Unfinished castle of King Ludwig II at about 965 m above Schwangau, on a rock over the Pöllat gorge.
tags: [Castle, UNESCO]
aliases: [Neuschwanstein, Neuschwanstein Castle]
---
Ludwig II had the ruins of two medieval castles, Vorderhohenschwangau and
Hinterhohenschwangau, blasted away in 1868 to make room for a new "knight's
castle" above his father's Hohenschwangau. The foundation stone was laid on
5 September 1869. The designs came from Christian Jank, a Munich theatre
painter, and were carried out by the architects Eduard Riedel, then Georg von
Dollmann from 1874 and Julius Hofmann from 1880, who drew the Throne Hall.

When Ludwig died on 13 June 1886 the castle was far from done. Only about 15
of the 200-odd planned rooms were finished, and the 90 m keep and the chapel
were never built. About six weeks later the Prince Regent Luitpold opened it to
paying visitors. Today it draws 1 to 1.5 million of them a year, it inspired
Disney's Sleeping Beauty Castle, and since July 2025 it is a UNESCO World
Heritage Site together with Linderhof, Schachen and Herrenchiemsee.

Just above the castle, the Marienbrücke spans the Pöllat gorge about 90 m over
the waterfall. Maximilian II built the first wooden bridge in 1845; Ludwig
replaced it with the iron one in 1866 and named it after his mother, Marie of
Prussia. It is the classic viewpoint for photos of the castle.

- **Visiting:** the inside can only be seen on a guided tour of about 30
  minutes (14 rooms), with tickets timed to the minute. Book online at
  hohenschwangau.de; the Ticket Center in Hohenschwangau sells only leftover
  same-day tickets.
- **Opening hours:** 9:00–18:00 from 28 March to 15 October, 10:00–16:00
  otherwise; closed 24, 25 and 31 December and 1 January.
- **Getting up:** 30–40 minutes on foot along a steep road from the Ticket
  Center, or by horse carriage from Hotel Müller, or by shuttle bus to the
  viewpoint near the Marienbrücke (not in snow or ice).
- **Rules:** no photos or filming inside, no rucksacks or prams on the tour,
  and no drones taking off or landing on the castle grounds.
