title: Baumgartenalm

The Baumgartenalm lies at 1368 m on open alm meadows on the ridge just north
of the Baumgartenschneid (1448 m), the mountain between the Tegernsee and the
Schliersee; one route guide gives its height as about 1350 m. It can be seen
from the summit cross. From the alm's first building the path turns south and
runs almost flat towards the summit block. To the north a pasture fence and a
small gate lead into the forest, and a steep path with many switchbacks drops
to the Sagfleckl (1154 m), the saddle towards the Kreuzbergköpfel, about
45 minutes below the summit.

Route guides from Tegernsee suggest coming down from the Baumgartenschneid
past the alm, a slightly longer and shadier alternative to the way up. From
the Sagfleckl, a forest road leads through the Alpbachtal back to Tegernsee
station, and another down to Breitenbach in Schliersee. Coming up from
Schliersee, the path from the Sagfleckl to the alm is steep and rough, and
slippery when wet.

- **Access:** from Tegernsee, over the Riederstein and the summit or through
  the Alpbachtal and the Sagfleckl; from Schliersee via the Sagfleckl.
- **Path:** steep, rough and slippery when wet between the alm and the
  Sagfleckl.
