The Tegernseer Hütte belongs to the DAV's Tegernsee section and sits on the
saddle between the summits of the Roßstein (1698 m) and the Buchstein
(1701 m), in the municipality of Kreuth. The first hut, 12 m² and built from
material carried up by hand, went up in autumn 1903 and opened on 14 August
1904. A larger hut followed in 1913, and a material cable car in 1958. On
10 May 1965 a lightning fire destroyed it, and the new hut was dedicated on
27 August 1967. It was renovated and extended in 2005/06, and rebuilt for
fire safety from May 2020, reopening on 3 June 2021. The hut has no spring:
it collects rainwater in a 15,000 l tank, filtered and UV-treated but not
drinkable, and gets its power from photovoltaics.

The steepest way up starts at the Roß- und Buchstein car park on the B 307
near Bayerwald: 4.5 km and about 900 m of ascent, 2 to 3 hours, past the
Sonnbergalm and the Brotzeitfelsen. The last part is either a short protected
climb with steel cables or the Altweibersteig around the north side, about
45 minutes longer. The gentler route from the Klamm/Schwarzentenn car park
follows the forest road past the Schwarzentennalm and the Buchsteinhütte,
about 8.5 km and 2.5 to 3.5 hours. The Roßstein summit is 10 to 15 minutes
from the hut. The Buchstein's normal route is a grade II gully without fixed
protection, and its walls hold around 100 climbing routes.

- **Open:** usually mid-May to early November; check the website.
- **Beds:** 21 dormitory places in two rooms; bring a hut sleeping bag.
- **Food:** dinner 18:00 to 19:00, breakfast buffet 7:30 to 9:00.
- **Access:** bus 9656 from Tegernsee station to Klamm/Schwarzentenn or
  Tegernseer Hütte; the car parks charge a fee, coins only.
- **Website:** [tegernseerhuette.de](https://tegernseerhuette.de/)
