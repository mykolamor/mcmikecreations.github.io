In the middle of the week, Annette posted an invite to a sledding trip at
Spitzingsee. I haven't done any sledding in years, so I was excited to join.
The weather was perfect for it, with clear skies and a good amount of snow on
the ground. And the group of students who joined were all very friendly and fun
to be around.

![Spitzingsee frozen over](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/spitzingsee_schlittenfahrt/2024-12-14-00.jpg)
![Going around Spitzingsee](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/spitzingsee_schlittenfahrt/2024-12-14-01.jpg)

The hike started off cheerfully, we were maintaining a good pace and enjoying
the scenery. There's plenty of ways to get to the top of the mountain, but we
took the nicely packed gravel road going up to Firstalm. Some participants
were a bit faster than others, so we naturally split into a few groups,
chatting along the way.

![View down at Untere Firstalm (1318 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/spitzingsee_schlittenfahrt/2024-12-14-02.jpg)

After we reached Untere Firstalm (1318 m), instead of taking the path up to the
higher hut, we took the path going straight up to the ridge. Annette wanted to
show the new participants the view from the ridge, and it was definitely worth
it!

![View over the ridge towards Risserkogel (1826 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/spitzingsee_schlittenfahrt/2024-12-14-03.jpg)

From the ridge, we went back down to the hut, and continued onwards to Obere
Firstalm (1369 m). After a quick toilet break at the hut, we decided to skip
the meal inside and instead rented a sled per person for the way down. The
sleds were quite new, making the ride down much more enjoyable. Surprisingly,
the path was very smooth and didn't have any bumps, which made it a lot of fun
to go down.

![Looking over Obere Firstalm (1369 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/spitzingsee_schlittenfahrt/2024-12-14-04.jpg)

After reaching the bottom first, overtaking the slower riders, I waited for the
others and saw a snowmobile taking the sleds back up to the top. It was cool
to see a bunch of sleds being pulled up at once.

Once everyone was at the bottom, we considered taking the bus back to Neuhaus,
but instead decided to walk down along the trails and waterfalls ourselves.
The way was simple and safe, the trail was quite clear of snow, and there
were a lot of other hikers on the way.

![Snowman on the way down](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/spitzingsee_schlittenfahrt/2024-12-14-08.jpg)

At one point, we needed to cross the river on a small bridge, and we noticed
a snowman sitting at the other side. Everyone was super excited, and we took a
lot of pictures with it. When we were going past Josefstaler Wasserfälle,
we stopped for a while to take in the view of the nature and the sound of the
water.

![Josefstaler Wasserfälle above](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/spitzingsee_schlittenfahrt/2024-12-14-05.jpg)
![Josefstaler Wasserfälle below](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/spitzingsee_schlittenfahrt/2024-12-14-06.jpg)

At the end, we arrived back at the train station and took the train back home.
It was a great day out, and I'm glad I got to experience sledding again after
so many years.

![A house on the way in Neuhaus](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/spitzingsee_schlittenfahrt/2024-12-14-07.jpg)

If you want to enjoy sledding in the Bavarian Alps, I highly recommend going to
Spitzingsee. The trail is well maintained and smooth, unlike others in Bad Tölz
or Oberaudorf. You can rent sleds at Obere Firstalm (1369 m), and the ride down
is a lot of fun.
