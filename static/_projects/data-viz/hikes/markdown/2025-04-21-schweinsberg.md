When we started the route together with Annette and DAAD scholars she assembled,
I didn't know where exactly we were going, only that it's close to Bayrischzell
and that we will be hunting for Easter eggs. I assumed the hike would be quite
easy because of the number of newcomers that joined us, but this wasn't the case.

![Veiw from the slope onto Rotwand (1884 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/schweinsberg/2025-04-21-00.jpg)

Although I wouldn't call it difficult, the gravel road up was quite steep, so
people were getting exhausted quite fast. On the other hand, we had a nice view
of [Aiplspitz (1759 m)](/hikes/2024-08-31-aiplspitz/) and [Rotwand (1884 m)](/hikes/2024-09-29-rotwand/)
which compensated our troubles.

As we continued up, we turned from the gravel road onto a dirt path between the
trees, which supposedly is part of the Medititationswnaderweg Fischbachau. We
came to an open area with some huts around and headed to the summit.

![Veiw from Türkenköpfl (1512 m) at Elbacher Kreuz (1510 m) and onto Schweinsberg (1514 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/schweinsberg/2025-04-21-01.jpg)

We reached the Elbacher Kreuz (1510 m) and went a few meters further to Türkenköpfl (1512 m),
took our photos and talked with some other hikers on the way to
[Wendelstein (1838 m)](/404/).

![View from Elbach Alm at Zettler-Hütte](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/schweinsberg/2025-04-21-02.jpg)

We turned back and went past Zettler-Hütte, going on the hillside until we reached a
saddle with a turn to Schweinsberg (1514 m). The whole side of the mountain was
covered in all sorts of beautiful flowers, including white crocuses, that covered
the grass in nice tiny patches.

![White crocus flowers on the way to Schweinsberg (1514 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/schweinsberg/2025-04-21-03.jpg)

I was also surprised that the sign at the saddle mentioned a wild skiing track going
east. I'm not sure where the people are supposed to ski, since there was a forest and
a water stream down below.

![Alpine choughs at Schweinsberg (1514 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/schweinsberg/2025-04-21-04.jpg)

As we arrived at the top of the second mountain, we were ambushed by alpine choughs,
and I had to give away my Beefy to them as reparation again, repeating events from
[Hochstaufen (1771 m)](/hikes/2025-03-09-hochstaufen/). We also came across
a group of russian hikers eating Easter bread and downing it with wine on the summit.

On the way down from the mountain we made a break in a nice grove and went Easter egg
hunting. We split into two teams and hid them for the other team, then switching to
search for them. The game ended in a draw and everyone left happy with a pile of
sweets!

After we took a bus to Miesbach, we still had some time before the train, so we went
to the Station's Kebap House and got some food for the road.
