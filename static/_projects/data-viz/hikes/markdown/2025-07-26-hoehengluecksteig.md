Topo from bergsteigen.com for [Norissteig](https://www.bergsteigen.com/touren/klettersteig/norissteig-klettersteig/)
and [Höhenglücksteig](https://www.bergsteigen.com/touren/klettersteig/hoehengluecksteig-klettersteig/):
![Topo of Norissteig](/images/hikes/topo/norissteig.png)
![Topo of Höhenglücksteig](/images/hikes/topo/hoehengluecksteig.png)

Mikuláš was coming back to Germany and wanted to do a hike together. We planned
to hike up Zugspitze, but the weather forecast predicted thunderstorms in the
afternoon, so we changed our plan to via ferrata climbing at Hohenglückstein, a
rock formation near Fischbrunn. By the way, I did hike Zugspitze with David
instead, going up the [Reintal](/hikes/2025-06-21-zugspitze_reintal/) and
[Höllental](/hikes/2025-09-18-zugspitze_hoellental/) routes.

![A building in Hersbruck](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/hoehengluecksteig/2025-07-26-00.jpg)
![The Hersbruck castle](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/hoehengluecksteig/2025-07-26-01.jpg)

The commute was very long and tiring, as we had to change transports in
Hersbruck, so we also got to explore the town a bit. We started our hike in
Fischbrunn, walking through a beautiful forest to the base of the Norissteig
via ferrata.

![Going to Norissteig](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/hoehengluecksteig/2025-07-26-02.jpg)
![Signing my visit to Norissteig](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/hoehengluecksteig/2025-07-26-06.jpg)

The climb was fun and easy, with some exposed sections and
beautiful views of the surrounding forest.

![The Norissteig via ferrata](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/hoehengluecksteig/2025-07-26-03.jpg)

After finishing the Norissteig, we took a short lunch break and then continued
along the cliffs to the Höhenglücksteig via ferrata. This climb was more
challenging, with some steep and exposed sections with smaller footholds. But
we managed to complete most of it without issues, only needing to skip the last
part due to time constraints.

![The Höhenglücksteig via ferrata](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/hoehengluecksteig/2025-07-26-04.jpg)
![Our climbing group](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/hoehengluecksteig/2025-07-26-07.jpg)

Then, we turned south-east and walked down through the forest to the village of
Neutras. The walk was peaceful and scenic, with nice views of the surrounding
hills and meadows. From Neutras, we kept walking to Etzelwang, where the train
station was located.

![Going through the meadows](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/hoehengluecksteig/2025-07-26-05.jpg)

Overall, it was a great day of hiking and climbing, with beautiful scenery and
fun challenges. I would definitely recommend the Norissteig and Höhenglücksteig
via ferratas to anyone looking for a fun and adventurous hike in the area. It's
a must-do for any via ferrata enthusiast!

P.S. I thank Mikuláš for joining me on this great trip and the photos he took
during the hike!
