The hike that Annette from DAAD FK organized started off disastrously, because
most of the group got confused on how to commute to Spitzingsee and either left
at the wrong train station or missed the bus. While the organizer was dealing
with the issue, I decided not to stand around with the group and went to the
nearest bakery to get a cake.

![Eating cake at Spitzingsee](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/auerspitz/2024-10-12-00.jpg)

By the time I finished it the rest of the group arrived, so we started the
hike. The initial walk was picturesque, going on the hillside along river
Rote Valepp. As soon as we turned east towards the mountains, the trail changed
completely, now going between riverbeds, hills, cliffs, and trees.

![Rote Valepp cliffside](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/auerspitz/2024-10-12-01.jpg)

Around this point, climbing on a narrow trail uphill towards the Alpine huts,
the group has started to stretch. The quicker hikers were waiting for the
slower ones, and Annette was making sure that nobody at any point is lost. At
one moment, a pair of international hikers approached our group and asked for
some photos, so we had something to do while waiting.

![View towards Bayerischer Schinder (1796 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/auerspitz/2024-10-12-02.jpg)
![Shepherd huts on the way](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/auerspitz/2024-10-12-03.jpg)

We passed some shepherd huts and started climbing the steeper section of the
route. We were getting close to the Rotwandhaus (1737 m), and it was nice to
see some civilization when looking up. I suggested making a stop at the hut,
but we already wasted an hour waiting for the group to gather initially, so it
made little sense to stop at the hut wasting more time.

![Rotwandhaus (1737 m) from below](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/auerspitz/2024-10-12-04.jpg)
![View towards Hinteres Sonnwendjoch (1986 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/auerspitz/2024-10-12-05.jpg)

After passing the Kümpflscharte (1695 m) saddle and reaching the
Auerspitz (1811 m) summit, some DAAD FK students brought out their cameras and
started taking photos of the scenery. I kept my binoculars on me, so I started
handing them out to others that wanted to see the surrounding mountains up
close. We even saw Rotwand (1884 m) that I [visited in September](/hikes/2024-09-29-rotwand/).

![View down from Auerspitz (1811 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/auerspitz/2024-10-12-06.jpg)
![View from Auerspitz (1811 m) towards Rotwand (1884 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/auerspitz/2024-10-12-09.jpg)

After relaxing on the peak for a bit and taking a group photo, we started
heading down to Bayrischzell. Walking past a giant cliff which turned out to be
Ruchenköpfe (1805 m), I was excited to climb it, but checking the map revealed
that the route up is better attempted with a rope and climbing gear. Maybe,
I'll be back for a faithful attempt in the future. At least I got to see
Seebergkopf (1538 m) that [DAAD FK went to in the past](/404/)
and Wendelstein (1838 m) I [visited a couple of times](/404/).

![View towards Soinsee from above](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/auerspitz/2024-10-12-08.jpg)
![Looking at Ruchenköpfe (1805 m) with Wendelstein (1838 m) in the background](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/auerspitz/2024-10-12-07.jpg)

We really split up while going to the Soinsee lake, so there was a longer wait
ahead. I went up to and around the lake, passing some snow and mud patches on
route. Thankfully, some people of the faster batch had cameras with them, so I
spent time looking at them fiddling with camera setting taking pictures of the
lake.

![Soinsee up close](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/auerspitz/2024-10-12-10.jpg)
![Soinsee with background cliffs](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/auerspitz/2024-10-12-11.jpg)

Eventually, we got to the crossroads and Annette decided to switch our target
and head to Geitau. This section of the hike is just a dirt road, so we walked
together without issues. We passed Segelflugplatz LSC Schliersee, a small
airfield for gliders, and I was surprised to see some gliders taking off and
landing there. The hike ended at the train station in Geitau, where we took the
train back to Munich after having a snack in a nearby beer garden.

![Gliders at Segelflugplatz LSC Schliersee](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/auerspitz/2024-10-12-12.jpg)
![Cows on the side of the road at Geitau](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/auerspitz/2024-10-12-13.jpg)

All in all, this is a great hike with a big variety of views and terrain.
Medium-difficulty, with some steeper sections but no climbing on all fours. The
way down to Soinsee is also fun, offering views of Seebergkopf (1538 m).
