Although I managed to avoid getting sick during my vacation, I could resist the
disease after my trip to GamesCom 2025. Having a 38.6°C fever and a clogged
nose, I had a brilliant idea to ignore my symptoms and go hiking with a mask
on.

![Spitzingsee](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/brecherspitz/2025-08-31-00.jpg)

Since Brecherspitz (1685 m) was in my to-do list ever since I saw it on my
[first Spitzingsee hike](/404/), I decided to go there. Annette warned me on
our [sledge riding trip](/hikes/2024-12-14-spitzingsee_schlittenfahrt/)
to Obere Firstalm (1369 m) that this path is
quite dangerous in winter, which implied it being also not too safe in summer,
so I knew I had to be extra careful, considering my sick state.

![View from Obere Firstalm (1369 m) towards Brecherspitz Vorgipfel (1643 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/brecherspitz/2025-08-31-01.jpg)

My usual plan for the 637a hiking trail involves getting a fast-paced start,
then slowing down to a normal pace around Untere Firstalm. This time, however,
was very different. Very quickly I realized that I can't keep up any decent
speed. As a solution, I decided to slow down to the speed of some senior hikers
along the way, keeping a significant distance to them to not get them sick.

![View down from the hill to Obere Firstalm (1369 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/brecherspitz/2025-08-31-02.jpg)

When I reached Untere Firstalm, I had a full-on nausea attack, having to catch
my breath and calm down in the bathroom.

After somehow passing Obere Firstalm (1369 m), I started climbing the
625 mountain trail towards Brecherspitz Vorgipfel (1643 m). It was an unusual
experience having to wait every two minutes to catch my breath away from the
trail, while elderly hikers walked past me without pausing.

![View from Brecherspitz Vorgipfel (1643 m) towards Schliersee](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/brecherspitz/2025-08-31-03.jpg)

The path further along to Brecherspitz (1685 m) turned out to be close to what
was described as dangerous. Although most of it is secured by a cable on one
side, having an accident or horrible luck can still get you tumbling down the
mountain wall.

![View from Brecherspitz Vorgipfel (1643 m) towards Brecherspitz (1685 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/brecherspitz/2025-08-31-04.jpg)

At one point, I was passed by an Asian hiker complaining to everyone that she
lost the cap on one of her hiking sticks. I shrugged and kept going to the
summit.

![Via ferrata towards Brecherspitz (1685 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/brecherspitz/2025-08-31-05.jpg)

Eventually I reached the peak, quickly took a few photos and moved away into a
corner to catch my breath again and recuperate. The view from the top was quite
nice, and made me realize that Ankel-Alm existed.

![View from the peak of Brecherspitz (1685 m) towards Schliersee](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/brecherspitz/2025-08-31-06.jpg)
![View from the peak of Brecherspitz (1685 m) towards Spitzingsee](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/brecherspitz/2025-08-31-07.jpg)

It was nice to be on a peak I kept on my wishlist, but I knew I had to go down
sooner or later. And with my sickness getting worse due to my stupidity of
doing sports in this state, I knew I didn't have much time.

![Rose hip, the fruit of a rose plant](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/brecherspitz/2025-08-31-08.jpg)

I headed further along the ridge to reach the Alm below, took my sweet time
climbing down, passing by quite a few families with children going to the top,
and made my way below. It was unusual to see kids on the route, as there are
some slippery rocks and narrow paths on the way, which most parents would deem
unacceptable. I also met a few Asian tourists taking photos while going up too.

![Descending slope of Brecherspitz (1685 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/brecherspitz/2025-08-31-07.jpg)

After getting to the hut and seeing the famous Panorama-Klo with a giant queue,
I headed along the gravel road back to Neuhaus and went back home to rest in
bed.

I would recommend this route to anyone in the area, as it's quite a different
experience compared to [Stümpfling (1506 m)](/hikes/2025-09-07-wasserspitz/)
and others I've been to
before. The views of the lake and other peaks, including
[Wendelstein (1838 m)](/404/), are great, giving you a sweet panorama to look
at. Just don't go there while sick, ok?
