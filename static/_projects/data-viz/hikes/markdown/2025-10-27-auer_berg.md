The day of the hike came quickly after going climbing, playing table tennis
and tennis. I have also finally passed my practical driving test and received
my license, for which I started taking lessons around the time of the hike from
[Herrsching to Tutzing](/hikes/2025-02-23-herrsching_tutzing/). Only took me 4600€!
Checking the weather forecast the day before, I decided
to wait until morning and see what happens. At 6:30 AM, it was raining
everywhere, so I fell back asleep. At 10:00 AM I finally got up and found out,
that the weather completely changed for the better. Thus, I packed some warm
clothes and set off to Schliersee to hike near the lake.

![Cascades on the way up](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/auer_berg/2025-10-27-00.jpg)
![Lush vegetation near the valley](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/auer_berg/2025-10-27-01.jpg)

Soon enough, however, I discovered that the train to Bayrischzell was
cancelled, so I desperately looked for a route near Tegernsee or Lenggries.
The following hike is the result.

![Mobile hunting blind near the hiking path](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/auer_berg/2025-10-27-02.jpg)
![Moss and trees closer to the top](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/auer_berg/2025-10-27-03.jpg)

Everything started off very easy, the road was wide, not steep, and accessible
to off-road vehicles. As I kept climbing higher, the scenery behind the trees
and the tree species themselves changed, but the road remained largely the
same.

![Fern moss](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/auer_berg/2025-10-27-04.jpg)
![Haircap moss](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/auer_berg/2025-10-27-05.jpg)

Near the top of [Gindelalmschneid (1335 m)](/404/) at Postner Hütte the nice road
finished, and what followed was a tiny trail with no official markings, going
along the ridge towards the peak.

![Gindelalmschneid (1335 m) peak](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/auer_berg/2025-10-27-06.jpg)
![Fly Agaric at the peak](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/auer_berg/2025-10-27-07.jpg)

As I reached the summit, the path forward was blocked off by barbed wire, with
no normal ways to cross. Going back and forth along the fence, I found a
shallower spot to climb over, but the whole situation with a risk to my pants
worsened my mood by a lot.

![View towards Wendelstein (1838 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/auer_berg/2025-10-27-08.jpg)
![View from Gindelalm back onto Gindelalmschneid (1335 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/auer_berg/2025-10-27-09.jpg)

As I reached the top, a nice familiar view towards Schliersee and
[Wendelstein (1838 m)](/hikes/2025-06-10-wendelstein/) opened up. From the top
of the mountain onwards to Gindelalm and Auer Berg (1252 m) the
way was pleasant and mostly uneventful.

![Rainer Berg (1169 m) peak](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/auer_berg/2025-10-27-10.jpg)

Then, I decided to leave the official route and visit Rainer Berg (1169 m).
Not to be confused with Rainerkopf (1463 m) near Spitzingsee, which I passed by
on my recent hike to [Wasserspitz (1552 m)](/hikes/2025-09-07-wasserspitz/).
Turns out this peak was so unpopular that there wasn't even a trail to it.
Going on a narrow muddy path first and climbing through bushes and fallen trees
next, I managed to reach the GPS location of the mountaintop only to see a tiny
stone and a branch stuck in the ground. Good enough, I guess.

![Naturerlebnis Huberspitz Wanderweg](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/auer_berg/2025-10-27-11.jpg)
![View of Hausham](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/auer_berg/2025-10-27-12.jpg)

Going down to Huberspitz (1051 m) and listening to creaking trees and rustling
leaves along the way, I came across Naturerlebnis Huberspitz Wanderweg, a nice
educational children trail with information about local nature. Since I didn't
prefer going to Schliersee over Hausham, I headed down this path to finish off
the hike.

![Monument to Bergwerk Hausham](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/auer_berg/2025-10-27-13.jpg)

Overall, the hike is nice and easy, but the only scenic viewpoint is
Gindelalmschneid (1335 m), the rest of the route is just going through a
forest. I would recommend sticking to [Maximiliansweg](/404/) instead for a more
rewarding experience.

![Doo-doo water with extra sugar](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/auer_berg/2025-10-27-14.jpg)
