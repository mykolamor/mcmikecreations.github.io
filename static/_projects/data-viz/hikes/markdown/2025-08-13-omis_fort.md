Since the weather in Croatia that day didn't allow for paragliding or
parasaling, I decided to do a small hike to a nearby summit. Since it looked
quite short, I set out to do the hike with my mom.

![View onto the fort from the town](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/omis_fort/2025-08-13-00.jpg)

The route starts going through the Omiš city centre with all the old
architecture. We started early, so we didn't get to see all the souvenir
and fake weed shops. Instead, we came across all the restaurant workers washing
the tables and chairs outdoors with hoses, getting us a bit splashed too.

![View at the amusement park near the main bridge](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/omis_fort/2025-08-13-01.jpg)

After passing through the town, we started going up past some villas, mostly on
stairs, but sometimes across dirt patches. Soon we reached a nice viewpoint
near the fire exit tunnel of the highway tunnel in the mountain, where we made
a stop to rest and hydrate.

![The beginning of the hike](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/omis_fort/2025-08-13-02.jpg)
![The view from the panoramic spot onto the coast](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/omis_fort/2025-08-13-03.jpg)

Soon we continued our way up, going over a gravel and sand path up the
mountain. The stones and gravel made the route quite slippery, especially for
the slippers we were wearing. It wasn't much of a problem going up, but we
already started mentally preparing for the rough way down.

![The view from the panoramic spot onto the city](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/omis_fort/2025-08-13-04.jpg)

As we kept ascending, some hikes passed us going up and down, and the mood
seemed great for everyone. Soon after we reached the last section of the trip,
a mostly flat walk on the wide and forested ridge of the mountain. I had a
stupid idea to take a detour alone to Vrh Pleća (489 m), but the lack of a
trail and the dry pointy vegetation made me drop this idea before I did
anything.

![A glimpse at the fortress](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/omis_fort/2025-08-13-05.jpg)
![Inside the fortress](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/omis_fort/2025-08-13-06.jpg)

Soon we got the Tvrđava "Stari grad" (303 m) in view, and we asked some
tourists to make some photos of us. It was then when I noticed some hikers
wearing helmets and klettersets for rock climbing. I decided to stay quiet at
that point, but I started getting some risky ideas (I had only slippers on me
on the trip, no climbing equipment at all).

![Greeting plaque in the fortress](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/omis_fort/2025-08-13-07.jpg)

We went into the fortress, paying quite a bit for the entrance. Got some nice
views, rested on the benches, climbed the tower and took photos and selfies in
every direction.

![Looking down from the fortress](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/omis_fort/2025-08-13-08.jpg)

After everything was done, we started heading down the same way. As we were
descending, we met quite a few different groups of tourists going up: families
with children, older or inactive people, trail runners. It was nice to see such
variety. We even met a group of hikers that came to the town by boat
specifically to go on this hike (but taking the rock climbing way up).

![View down towards the via ferrata from the fortress](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/omis_fort/2025-08-13-09.jpg)

Further down, our suspicion regarding the slippery rocks came true. As both my
mom and me slipped quite a few times. Thankfully, apart from getting a bit
dusty, nobody was hurt.

![The interior of the fortress](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/omis_fort/2025-08-13-10.jpg)

Eventually we reached the bottom of the trail and headed home. We had another
walk across the town, now filled with tourist traps and people waking up and
heading for breakfast around noon, and went by the seaside to have one last
look at the mountain from the bottom up.

![A signpost on the way down from the fortress](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/omis_fort/2025-08-13-11.jpg)

If you're ever in the area, you have to check out this route. It is easy and
accessible to people of all ages, and the views from the top are great.
