The group of Ukrainians I've met at the end of my recent hike to
[Auer Berg (1252 m)](/hikes/2025-10-27-auer_berg/)
invited me to an outing up Jochberg, which I visited a couple of times before.
With a fractured finger and nothing better to do, I gladly accepted the
invitation and joined the group. We set out early in the morning, traveling in
the direction of Kochel.

![Lainbachfall](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/lainbachfall_jochberg/2025-12-14-00.jpg)

At the station we changed our initial plan and decided to also visit the
[Lainbachfall waterfall](/404/)
and [Sonnenspitz (1269 m)](/404/).
Although not everyone in the group was equally fit, we decided to stick to the
new plan.

![Lower Lainbachfall](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/lainbachfall_jochberg/2025-12-14-01.jpg)

Our way to the waterfall was largely uneventful. The first chunk of the road
was very slippery, since all the fog and cold air were freezing the water
droplets on the ground and asphalt, making it a perfect slide.

Closer to the waterfall the road was paved with gravel and dirt, so walking
became much easier. We came to the source of the waterfall and took a bunch of
photos, taking a longer break in the meantime.

![Kienstein (968 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/lainbachfall_jochberg/2025-12-14-02.jpg)

Once we finished resting, we headed up to Sonnenspitz (1269 m). The route there
is really nice, going near Kienstein (968 m). Although now is not the time for
it, you can see many rock climbers with full gear going there to practice
climbing up the cliff.

![View towards Dachsenberg (1061 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/lainbachfall_jochberg/2025-12-14-03.jpg)

On the way up one can find a lot of scenic viewpoints. Given appropriate
weather, each one of them is worth it. With the clouds covering the valley and
falling over the [Walchensee Kraftwerk](/404/),
the view is really breathtaking.

![View towards Walchensee Kraftwerk](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/lainbachfall_jochberg/2025-12-14-04.jpg)

Once we reached the top, we realized where the mountain got its name from. It's
sunny for most of the day, even in winter. We stayed at the summit for quite
some time, having small talk and enjoying the views. It was also nice to see a
lot of other hikers on the route, some bringing dogs. The view towards
[Herzogstand (1731 m)](/404/)
was amazing, with the valley in the clouds, and the mountains illuminated by
the sun at its highest.

![Kotalm plateau](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/lainbachfall_jochberg/2025-12-14-05.jpg)

We kept going down and reached a plateau going in the direction of Walchensee.
It was a nice reprieve going on flat terrain, but our enjoyment was cut short
by the next section through a tree-covered mountain side. While the snow down
below was hardened by the sun, the hill had waterfalls with fresh running water
and puffy snow covered in the shade of spruce trees. It didn't seem like a lot
of people took that route, but it was still fully walkable for a group of
various fitness levels.

![Hunting spot on the way](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/lainbachfall_jochberg/2025-12-14-06.jpg)
![Kotalm](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/lainbachfall_jochberg/2025-12-14-07.jpg)

Eventually we reached the snowy and muddy valley of Jocheralm. We could already
see the peak of Jochberg (1565 m) from there, making us enthusiastic about the
way ahead.

![View from Jocheralm up to Jochberg (1565 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/lainbachfall_jochberg/2025-12-14-08.jpg)
![View from Jochberg (1565 m) towards Walchensee](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/lainbachfall_jochberg/2025-12-14-09.jpg)

We started the final section and reached the peak just 20 minutes later. The
vista from the top was mind-blowing. The clouds in the Kochel region contrast
the clear skies of Walchensee, [Simetsberg (1840 m)](/404/)
in the distance in front of [Zugspitze (2962 m)](/hikes/2025-06-21-zugspitze_reintal/),
and [Benediktenwand (1800 m)](/hikes/2024-07-21-benediktenwand/)
in the other direction add up to an amazing panorama. The tourists were also
really kind, patiently stomaching our chatter the whole time we stayed there.

![View from Jochberg (1565 m) towards Kochelsee](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/lainbachfall_jochberg/2025-12-14-10.jpg)

After enjoying the view to the fullest, we started heading down the usual way
to Urfeld. It was already getting dark, but we still managed to enjoy the
Walchensee beach for a bit. Reaching the final destination, we took the bus
and headed back home. While waiting for the bus, we saw two guys and a girl
exit a car they parked nearby. They took off all their clothes and jumped into
the lake for a quick swim. It was quite a sight to see in the cold evening.

![Walchensee up close in the winter evening](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/lainbachfall_jochberg/2025-12-14-11.jpg)

I'd highly recommend this route to anyone interested in starting to hike. It
can be done in sections (waterfall only, Sonnenspitz (1269 m), and
Jochberg (1565 m)), so it's accessible to people of most fitness levels. In
summer the hut is supposedly open, so you can even rest there. The views
towards Kochelsee and Walchensee are nice in any weather, and on a clear day
you might get a chance to see Zugspitze (2962 m) too!
