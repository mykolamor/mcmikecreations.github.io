With the new DAAD scholars arriving in Munich for their studies, we wanted to
give them a taste of the local nature and culture. Annette, another DAAD FK
member, sent out an invitation to a hike in the Karwendel mountains, and the
group was happy to join.

I wanted to argue that normally an easier hike is chosen for newcomers, but
looking at other posts on my blog, I realized that Annette also has a tendency
to choose more challenging hikes like [Auerspitz (1811 m)](/hikes/2024-10-12-auerspitz/).
Nevertheless, I joined and was ready to help.

The hike started at the Laliderertal bus stop and parking lot, where we left
the transport and prepared for the ascent. There was a short trail section with
a fallen over tree, which we had to climb over, but it was not too difficult.

![The fallen tree near the Laliderertal bus stop](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/mahnkopf/2026-10-11-00.jpg)
![View from the road at Unterer Roßkopf (1814 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/mahnkopf/2026-10-11-01.jpg)

After the initial climb, the trail led us to a wide gravel road leading to the
huts above. This section has a very shallow incline, making it accessible for
bikes, strollers, and anything in-between. The group was mostly chatting with
each other, while I made sure everyone was keeping up.

![A look back at the Laliderertal valley](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/mahnkopf/2026-10-11-02.jpg)
![Approaching the huts under Laliderer Wände](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/mahnkopf/2026-10-11-03.jpg)

Once we reached the huts under the Laliderer Wände (the wall of cliffs towering
above us), we took a break and enjoyed the view. So far, everyone was doing
well, so we started considering going to Mahnkopf (2093 m) instead of just
stopping at Falkenhütte (1846 m). Nobody seemed to mind, and we continued.

![The group posing at a chapel near the huts under Laliderer Wände](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/mahnkopf/2026-10-11-04.jpg)
![Going up to Falkenhütte (1846 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/mahnkopf/2026-10-11-05.jpg)

The road here turns east and leads straight to the Hohljoch (1794 m) saddle,
which in turn leads down to Eng. Instead, we turned west onto a narrow mountain
trail leading to our target. The trail was steeper and more challenging than
the gravel road, but it was still manageable for the group, and soon after we
reached Falkenhütte (1846 m).

![View from Falkenhütte (1846 m) towards Hohljoch (1794 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/mahnkopf/2026-10-11-06.jpg)
![Foggy view at Ladizköpfl (1920 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/mahnkopf/2026-10-11-07.jpg)

After a longer break at the hut, we continued to Mahnkopf (2093 m). This
optional section has three different routes: two around Ladizköpfl (1920 m)
and one going over it. We split up, and I took the route with the extra summit
to add a bit of challenge. Although by the time we reached the summit, the
clouds had rolled in and covered the view, it was still cool to be there.

![The road from Ladizköpfl (1920 m) to Mahnkopf (2093 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/mahnkopf/2026-10-11-08.jpg)
![The climb up to Mahnkopf (2093 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/mahnkopf/2026-10-11-09.jpg)

From there we reached the Ladizjöchl (1825 m) saddle, waited for the rest of
our group taking the longer route around, and then started climbing the main
slope to the peak. This is definitely the most challenging part of the hike,
with fast elevation gain, loose pebbles, and some slippery dirt patches. But
the group did well, and we all reached the final stretch with a dirt path to
the Mahnkopf (2093 m) summit.

![The dirt path to the cross at Mahnkopf (2093 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/mahnkopf/2026-10-11-10.jpg)
![Our DAAD FK hiking group resting on Mahnkopf (2093 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/mahnkopf/2026-10-11-11.jpg)

Finishing the ascent, we took another break at the summit and appreciated the
accomplishment. Unfortunately, because of the clouds, there was no view to be
seen, but we still felt good about the hike. After a while, we started the
descent back to Falkenhütte (1846 m), naturally splitting into a faster and a
slower subgroup.

![A mushroom on the way down from Mahnkopf (2093 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/mahnkopf/2026-10-11-12.jpg)
![Mountain goats near Ladizköpfl (1920 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/mahnkopf/2026-10-11-13.jpg)

We got back to the alpine hut safely and proceeded across a stone path to the
Hohljoch (1794 m) saddle. Although the path appeared quite flat both from below
and above, it was actually a bit of a climb, and we had to put in some effort
to get there. Along the way, we saw some mountain goats several times on the
slopes of Ladizköpfl (1920 m) and Mahnkopf (2093 m).

![View from the pass towards Hohljoch (1794 m) onto the valley](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/mahnkopf/2026-10-11-14.jpg)

After we passed the saddle, the way down was quite straightforward. Everyone
still had energy left, but was hungry, so we started planning a stop at one
of the restaurants in Eng for a meal. Although the trail crosses some streams
on the way down, there's nothing challenging there, and we reached the valley
floor without any issues.

![View onto the Enger-Grund-Bach stream valley](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/mahnkopf/2026-10-11-15.jpg)

The last stretch to Eng was not as steep, so we hurried down to the huts in
smaller subgroups and met up near the restaurants. We had a great cheese
platter and some drinks, and then we took the bus back to Lenggries. It was
the last local bus of the season, so I was happy to be here on this day
instead of taking one of the more popular routes to the Alps.

![View from the trail towards Eng](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/mahnkopf/2026-10-11-16.jpg)

All in all, it was a great day with a nice group of people, and I hope they
enjoyed their first hike in the Bavarian Alps. I look forward to more hikes
with them in the future! As for others planning to do this hike, I would
recommend it for everyone with good leg muscles and a bit of stamina. The
views are great if the weather is good, and the hike is on par with other
20 km hikes in the area. Just check the bus schedule at home!
