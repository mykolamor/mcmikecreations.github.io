This hike was organized by Annette from DAAD-Freundeskreis after quite a long
break. I decided to tag along, since I hadn't made any plans due to the public
holiday. The weather was insanely good too, 16.7°C in November, so I couldn't
make any excuses. We gathered at the station and quickly realized that we
weren't the only ones with an idea to go hiking. The train was full to the
brim, everyone was standing. Still, we ignored the signs and boarded the
section of the train to Schliersee.

![Obere Firstalm (1369 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/bodenschneid/2025-11-01-00.jpg)

After taking the train and a bus to Spitzingsee, we headed up to
Obere Firstalm (1369 m), from which we took the [sledges down last winter](/hikes/2024-12-14-spitzingsee_schlittenfahrt/),
and took a short break. I also changed my clothes,
since I got myself severely overprepared and overdressed.

![Items in need of a refill](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/bodenschneid/2025-11-01-02.jpg)
![Route up from the alm](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/bodenschneid/2025-11-01-03.jpg)
![Some planks to walk over the mud](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/bodenschneid/2025-11-01-04.jpg)

The rest of the way up was not as pleasant. The recent rain and snowfall turned
the whole mountainside into a consistent slush of mud, puddles, and dirt. Every
step we made got us dirtier, the slipping made us slower, and the splashes got
water into our shoes.

![Crossroads near Bodenschneid (1669 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/bodenschneid/2025-11-01-05.jpg)
![Small spruce sapling growing from a tree trunk](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/bodenschneid/2025-11-01-06.jpg)
![View from Bodenschneid (1669 m) onto Bodenschneidhaus (1365 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/bodenschneid/2025-11-01-07.jpg)

As we reached the plateau at the top, all of us, and random passer-bys,
repeated the same phase: "The route was horrible, we're all dirty and the
rubber profile of our shoes is non-existent". In any case, we reached the top
of Bodenschneid (1669 m), took a lot of group photos, went to the cross and
enjoyed the panorama. It really has some excellent views of Spitzingsee,
Schliersee, Tegernsee and München on the horizon. Then we started heading down
on the other side of the mountain.

![View from Bodenschneid (1669 m) onto Spitzingsee](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/bodenschneid/2025-11-01-08.jpg)
![Bodenschneid (1669 m) peak](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/bodenschneid/2025-11-01-09.jpg)

The other side wasn't much better, and going down made the chance of slipping
even higher. At some point I split from the group and headed to
Rinnerspitz (1611 m), since I skipped it on a [recent hike to Wasserspitz (1552 m)](/hikes/2025-09-07-wasserspitz/).

![Guelder rose](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/bodenschneid/2025-11-01-10.jpg)

The road from Bodenschneid (1669 m) to it was relatively ok. I got to take a
few photos and was practically running uphill. The way down, however, was much
more challenging. The same mud  covered every surface as before, but the path
was also incredibly steep. A few times I nearly fell down trying to gently
climb down a 1.0-1.5 meter drop.

![Rinnerspitz (1611 m) from the ridge](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/bodenschneid/2025-11-01-11.jpg)

I've reached the Georg Jennerwein memorial halfway to Wasserspitz (1552 m) that
I've [been to before](/hikes/2025-09-07-wasserspitz/) and started going down to
Bodenschneidhaus (1365 m). There I reconnected with the group (turns out I
arrived 5-6 minutes later than the others). We had some food and drinks and
headed down to Neuhaus.

![View of Bodenschneid (1669 m) from Rinnerspitz (1611 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/bodenschneid/2025-11-01-12.jpg)

To sum up, it was a good route with some nice views of the surrounding lakes
and peaks, suitable for beginners and intermediate hikers, but I would suggest
taking it only in good weather.

P.S. Due to an SEV bus we ended up in Schliersee and tried going to 
[Jianni's Greek Restaurant](https://maps.app.goo.gl/9huksxPbKzraRjRu7). The waiter
ignored our group of 4 people for 20 minutes, then told us they were only allowing
people with reservations. This wasn't true, as we saw several walk-in customers
getting seated. I told him about it (in German) and he suggested me to leave.
Another customer, watching German football at the table near the entrance, shouted
at me to go back to my home country. If you check other reviews online, I'm by far
not the only one experiencing this kind of behavior at this restaurant. Avoid it
at all costs. The publicly available restaurant phone number is [08026 922480](tel:+498026922480).
