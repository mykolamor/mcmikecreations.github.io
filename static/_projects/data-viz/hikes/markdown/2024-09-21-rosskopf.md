With the unusual trip to [Simetsberg (1840 m)](/hikes/2024-09-07-simetsberg/) behind me, I was ready to
have some fun in around the Bavarian lakes. I recalled seeing some signs about
mountain carts on the way back from [Aiplspitz (1759 m)](/hikes/2024-08-31-aiplspitz/),
so it seemed like a good idea to try them out. Besides, my flatmate Tony wanted
to try hiking with me, so I wanted to suggest a decent and simple route as an
introduction to the Bavarian Alps for him.

![View from Spitzingsee deeper in the mountains near Albert-Link-Hütte (1053 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/rosskopf/2024-09-21-00.jpg)

Although it snowed a few days before for no reason, I decided to go through
with the plan and send out an invite to other potential members. Dennis, a
recurring hiker and a DAAD FK member, also agreed to tag along. Serdar, another
DAAD FK member, also joined. We started the route walking deeper into the Alps
from Spitzingsee until we reached the Albert-Link-Hütte (1053 m).

![Untere Haushamer Alm with Rotwand (1884 m) in the background](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/rosskopf/2024-09-21-01.jpg)

From that point on, we turned right onto the slope and walked the forest road
all the way to Untere Haushamer Alm. I noticed the group split, with me and
Dennis in the front and the others falling behind. Thankfully, the route was
quite short, so time wasn't an issue and we rested whenever necessary.

![Walking up to Stolzenberg (1609 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/rosskopf/2024-09-21-02.jpg)

After the hut the road turned into a narrow trail with no prior footsteps. We
decided to keep going uphill following the rough direction of
Stolzenberg (1609 m), even having to climb over a giant fallen tree. The
road got more and more snowy as we kept climbing higher, until the snow reached
our ankles. The hikers I invited weren't too happy about this, but they decided
to keep pushing forward, hoping for the situation to get better.

![View from Stolzenberg (1609 m) towards Auerspitz (1811 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/rosskopf/2024-09-21-03.jpg)

When we reached the summit of Stolzenberg (1609 m), there was no cross waiting
for us, just a small pinned plate letting us know we reached the top. I wasn't
too upset by the situation, since it was the first mountaintop on our way, but
I can imagine someone targeting it as the final destination and being upset.
At least I got to see Auerspitz (1811 m) from there, which I [plan to visit](/hikes/2024-10-12-auerspitz/)
in the future.

![A sign at the top of Stolzenberg (1609 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/rosskopf/2024-09-21-04.jpg)
![View from Stolzenberg (1609 m) towards Rotkopf (1602 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/rosskopf/2024-09-21-05.jpg)

I've started hearing some complaining regarding the deep snow, so I proposed
descending from Stolzenberg (1609 m) down to Grünsee and turning back, but the
group got together and chose to keep going. We had Rotkopf (1602 m) as a clear
visible goal ahead of us, making it a bit more bearable by making us motivated.

![View from Rotkopf (1602 m) towards Brecherspitz (1685 m) and Spitzingsee](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/rosskopf/2024-09-21-06.jpg)
![A mushroom on the way](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/rosskopf/2024-09-21-07.jpg)

We kept going from Rotkopf (1602 m) onwards, and the ridge opened up to show us
a lot of nice views left and right. The most memorable was Brecherspitz (1685 m)
up ahead, looking like a perfect destination for a [future hike](/hikes/2025-08-31-brecherspitz/).

![Walking towards Roßkopf (1579 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/rosskopf/2024-09-21-08.jpg)
![A cross on the way to Roßkopf (1579 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/rosskopf/2024-09-21-09.jpg)

The last section of the ridge to Roßkopf (1579 m) goes directly above Grünsee.
We saw a lot of hikers take this route to hike straight up to the Jagahütt'n (1506 m)
restaurant near the summit, making my participants a bit jealous about having
to take the longer route there. We also came across a tiny cross tied to a
dried-up tree.

![View from Roßkopf (1579 m) towards Jägerkamp (1746 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/rosskopf/2024-09-21-10.jpg)

Once we got to the top, we took a short snack break and enjoyed the views. We
got to see Jägerkamp (1746 m) to the north-east, which I visited during my
[Aiplspitz (1759 m)](/hikes/2024-08-31-aiplspitz/) hike. To the south-west, we saw
Risserkogel (1826 m), a tougher peak accessible in summer, and
[Setzberg (1706 m)](/hikes/2026-01-11-setzberg/)
leading to [Wallberg (1722 m)](/404/). Serdar decided to end the trip there and
headed down alone.

![Descending from Roßkopf (1579 m) towards Jagahütt'n (1506 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/rosskopf/2024-09-21-11.jpg)

We paid for the mountain cart reservation and signed the agreement agreeing to
the terms and conditions of riding them. After standing in the queue for 15
minutes, we rode down the mountain overtaking each other, unknown drivers, and
random hikers trying to go uphill on the mountain cart route.

![View from Jagahütt'n (1506 m) towards Risserkogel (1826 m) and Setzberg (1706 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/rosskopf/2024-09-21-12.jpg)

When I made it down, I stood there waiting for the others, but nobody arrived.
After quite a while, the others appeared from around the corner. Turns out,
both Dennis and Tony managed to fly off the edge of the road on one of the
turns. Tony got off quite well but got stuck in some sticks. Dennis, on the
other hand, fell further, got some scratches, and got trapped in bushes. He
had to climb out of the cart and drag the vehicle up the hill back onto the
road. Thankfully, nothing major happened, and everyone left the area satisfied
with the trip.

All in all, the hike is great and quite appropriate for hikers of all levels
in any weather given the appropriate gear. I advise beginner hikers to skip
some of the summits on the route or take the Untere Firstalm route across
Stümpfling (1506 m) instead, as that one is wider and accessible for strollers.
If you decide to ride the mountain carts down in summer, or go to Obere
Firstalm (1369 m) to [ride sleds in winter](/hikes/2024-12-14-spitzingsee_schlittenfahrt/),
make sure to follow the safety instructions and be careful on the turns.
