---
I love the Ammergau region. But the connection to get there, especially from my
small town near Freising, is horrendous. This didn't stop me though. I called
Bohdan, who supervised me [breaking my finger](/hikes/2025-11-23-vordere_kesselschneid/)
near Pyramidenspitze (1997 m), and we set off early in the morning to arrive at
Unterammergau around 9:15 AM and start the hike to the Pürschlinghaus.

![Nice mural on a house in Unterammergau](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/teufelstaettkopf/2026-05-10-00.jpg)
![Decorative water mills near Unterammergau](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/teufelstaettkopf/2026-05-10-01.jpg)
![Josefs Kapelle on the way to August-Schuster-Haus](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/teufelstaettkopf/2026-05-10-02.jpg)

Although since the [climb to Kofel (1342 m)](/hikes/2026-05-03-kofel/)
I got my stiches removed on my wisdom tooth holes, I was still anxious about
overexerting myself. A few days earlier I slept on the wrong side of my face
and ever since my lip started to go numb from time to time (I went to the
doctor the day after and he told me it's fine and the numbness will go away).
With the troubles I kept having, I tried to be extra careful while not limiting
myself to a rudimentary beginner hike. The solution was to choose a normal
route but to take my time and walk smoothly.

![Mykola catching up to Bohdan](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/teufelstaettkopf/2026-05-10-04.jpg)
![A cross at the crossroads near August-Schuster-Haus](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/teufelstaettkopf/2026-05-10-03.jpg)
![View over the Pürschling (1566 m) ridge towards Zugspitze (2962 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/teufelstaettkopf/2026-05-10-05.jpg)

Around the halfway point of going up, Bohdan decided to speed up a bit and
train by hiking up faster, so he left me behind and I got to the top a while
later. Despite me taking my time, I still overtook some pairs and families
of hikers going to the hut for a lunch snack. Walking past the spot where
I [fell off a cliff](/hikes/2026-02-15-oberammergau_sonnenberg/) was eerie,
but I also remembered the
[adventure of retrieving the lost hiking pole](/hikes/2026-03-22-unterammergau_sonnenberg/)
and recovered my mood.

![Bohdan standing on top of Pürschling (1566 m) west peak](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/teufelstaettkopf/2026-05-10-06.jpg)
![Mykola climbing down Pürschling (1566 m) east peak](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/teufelstaettkopf/2026-05-10-07.jpg)

Near the hut, I met back with Bohdan and we did a small detour to
Pürschling (1566 m). It's a cool cliff overseeing the Linder valley, but
the official peak is on a scary-looking west peak section with no climbing
supports. While Bohdan climbed it and got down pretty fast, I decided to save
my nerves and stayed on the easier section of the east peak.

![Looking down onto the August-Schuster-Haus](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/teufelstaettkopf/2026-05-10-08.jpg)
![Multiple mountain flowers at Laubeneck (1520 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/teufelstaettkopf/2026-05-10-09.jpg)

Afterwards, we didn't linger near the August-Schuster-Haus for too long and
kept going further up. I got to see the snowmobile that took me down after
my fall though. The route towards Teufelstättkopf (1758 m) was pretty nice,
with some steeper sections in the shade of spruce trees and some shallow
sections with wooden bridges across muddy spots.

![Gentiana orbicularis near Laubeneck (1520 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/teufelstaettkopf/2026-05-10-11.jpg)

We again did a detour to Latschenkopf (1634 m) for completeness sake, spending
some time looking for the spot that counted as a summit. Once we were satisfied
with our location, we counted the peak as visited and turned around. This peak
offers a great view of Laubeneck (1520 m) right beside it, but we weren't
planning to visit it this time around.

![Laubeneck (1520 m) with Bohdan on top](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/teufelstaettkopf/2026-05-10-10.jpg)
![View from Laubeneck (1520 m) towards Laubeneck (1520 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/teufelstaettkopf/2026-05-10-12.jpg)

The route to Teufelstättkopf (1758 m) was also cool, with a tiny cable-secured
section towards the top. Although the polished rocks were slippery, the route
was easily accessible by hikers of all age groups. I even saw an older lady
with the same hiking poles as mine!

![The cable leading up to Teufelstättkopf (1758 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/teufelstaettkopf/2026-05-10-14.jpg)

The view from the top of Teufelstättkopf (1758 m) is great. You get a better
vantage point for looking at [Zugspitze (2962 m)](/hikes/2025-06-21-zugspitze_reintal/),
a full view of Sonnengrat, a distant mirage of [Wank (1780 m)](/404/), and
all the other summits you expect to see from this area.
If I'm not mistaken, from Teufelstättkopf (1758 m) you can even see
[Große Klammspitze (1924 m)](/404/), which is a great high summit to visit for
experienced hikers wishing to see Castle Linderhof!

![View from Teufelstättkopf (1758 m) towards Latschenkopf (1634 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/teufelstaettkopf/2026-05-10-15.jpg)
![A sharp turn of the ridge between Teufelstättkopf (1758 m) and Auf dem Stein (1579 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/teufelstaettkopf/2026-05-10-16.jpg)

We got down, had a drink and some snacks, and started our way down to
Unterammergau across all the neighboring summits. First came the ridge path
towards Auf dem Stein (1579 m). Despite how it looks on the map, the route is
quite safe and many people take it both ways throughout the day. I distinctly
remember how stuffy the air feels here. I guess all the warm air from both
valleys below slams into the hillside of this ridge and rises up.

![The ridge leading to Auf dem Stein (1579 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/teufelstaettkopf/2026-05-10-17.jpg)
![Auf dem Stein (1579 m) from above](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/teufelstaettkopf/2026-05-10-18.jpg)

When you get closer to the rock itself, you have to do a bit of easy rock
climbing, and you reach the top without even noticing. Although the view from
the rock is not as picturesque, the accomplishment of getting there feels much
better!

![Mykola climbing down Auf dem Stein (1579 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/teufelstaettkopf/2026-05-10-19.jpg)

It took me a while to get down to the amusement of Bohdan, but at last we were
back on track to Unterammergau. The path to the next summit was a typical
mountainside forest trail with roots and occasional pebbles here and there.
When I got home, I noticed my feet were fully covered in sand, and this section
was the likely source of it.

![Primula balbisii also known as Petergstamm near the rock](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/teufelstaettkopf/2026-05-10-20.jpg)
![A snail on the way down](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/teufelstaettkopf/2026-05-10-21.jpg)

Bremeneck (1354 m), the next summit, was a pretty simple flat mountaintop with
a bunch of hikers taking respite in the middle of their journey. Despite the
snake warnings, people were making shortcuts across the tall grass all over the
place, while Bohdan led me through the official trail. Still, I managed to
catch a tick somewhere here, which made for a fun time at home extracting it
from my skin.

![The snake warning on the way to Bremeneck (1354 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/teufelstaettkopf/2026-05-10-22.jpg)
![Mykola near the cross at Bremeneck (1354 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/teufelstaettkopf/2026-05-10-23.jpg)

We kept going to Rosengarten (1425 m), the low summit with a great view of
Sonnenberg (1622 m). Bohdan took a few photos while I was trying to catch up,
and we headed onward and downward.

![The cross at Rosengarten (1425 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/teufelstaettkopf/2026-05-10-24.jpg)

At this point, Bohdan asked me again regarding my hiking plan. While the
original idea was to visit two more summits, we had around 1.5 hours to the
next train, and my lip started to get numb again. I gave it some thought, but
ultimately decided to try making it on time. Worst case, I was ready to let
Bohdan run ahead and catch the train at his usual speed, while I would slowly
descend and take the next one.

![View from Rosengarten (1425 m) towards Sonnenberg (1622 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/teufelstaettkopf/2026-05-10-25.jpg)

At Schartenköpfl (1372 m), we didn't rest at all. Bohdan managed to snap a few
pictures on the way, I roughly followed him, and we continued down. At this
point we were taking trails through the forest, some of which were hard to see,
so at certain spots we just guessed the direction and made shortcuts.

![The registry box at Schartenköpfl (1372 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/teufelstaettkopf/2026-05-10-26.jpg)
![The cross at Schartenköpfl (1372 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/teufelstaettkopf/2026-05-10-27.jpg)

Closer to the next summit near Scharten-Kapelle, we entered a wider gravel road
used in forestry. It took us just a few more minutes to reach Köpfel (1194 m)
to complete our challenge. There wasn't even a stick in the ground to denote
the formal peak, but we were satisfied with being in the general area.

![The emptiness of Köpfel (1194 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/teufelstaettkopf/2026-05-10-28.jpg)

Bohdan wanted to do another quick section going north, since we had 50 minutes
left. I wasn't so confident in myself, so he suggested taking the shorter route
to Schleifmühlenkapelle and follow the rural road to the train station instead.
I followed his advice and went down, making some wrong turns here and there.

![The road Bohdan took towards Unterammergau](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/teufelstaettkopf/2026-05-10-29.jpg)

Despite coming across tons of hikers on the way, I didn't see anyone until I
reached the village. As I was passing local horses and cows, I met a nice
farm cat relaxing on a bench. I haven't even touched him when he started to
purr, so I had to scratch him at least a little bit as a reward. Since I was
in a rush to catch the train, I had to part ways with the cat, but I'll make
sure to grab some sausage snack next time around so I can treat him if he's
there!

![A cat in Unterammergau](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/teufelstaettkopf/2026-05-10-30.jpg)

Overall, based on survey evidence, this hike is good for hikers with average
experience regardless of age. I saw children, teenagers, their parents and
grandparents all throughout the hike, so it must be quite accessible. I'd
say it's on the more challenging side, maybe other people only do a portion
of the total route, but the area and the summits are definitely worth
checking out!
