I planned to do a Hausham gauntlet, visiting all named peaks around the town
to check them off my to-do list. As I was going to sleep, I checked my messages
and saw the invite in the "Wandern für glückliche Menschen" group for a hike to
Bolsterlanger Horn (1586 m).

![Chapel of St. Maria und St. Ottilia](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/bolsterlanger_horn_only/2026-01-25-00.jpg)
![At the bottom ski station](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/bolsterlanger_horn_only/2026-01-25-01.jpg)

Although I was hesitant at first because of the long commute, I decided to join
the group. After a few public transport changes, Natalia, me, and Kateryna
arrived in Fischen im Allgäu and took a bus to Bolsterlang. From there, we
started our hike going uphill on the side of a ski slope.

![On the way up](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/bolsterlanger_horn_only/2026-01-25-02.jpg)

The weather was perfect for hiking despite the forecast predicting rain and
snow later in the day. The slope started with an asphalt road leading up. Most
of the road was covered in ice, so we had to take the curbs to avoid slipping.
As we ascended, the view of the valley opened up, revealing the picturesque
villages below.

![Air balloon near Sonthofen](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/bolsterlanger_horn_only/2026-01-25-03.jpg)
![View from Hörnerhaus](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/bolsterlanger_horn_only/2026-01-25-04.jpg)

Soon, we reached the Hörnerbahn Mittelstation "s'Mibbadin", a mountain hut beer
garden located halfway up the mountain. It was bustling with people enjoying
the day, taking their skiing gear off to relax. The ladies asked for a short
break here before continuing our ascent. This hut, as far as I could tell, was
part of the Hörnerhaus resort.

![Sign at the "s'Mibbadin"](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/bolsterlanger_horn_only/2026-01-25-05.jpg)
![Snow machinery at the Hörnerhaus](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/bolsterlanger_horn_only/2026-01-25-06.jpg)

From the Hörnerhaus, the trail became steeper and more challenging. We took the
more direct route up the mountainsifer, because we didn't want to walk up the
active ski slope. The snow was deep, and I had to be extra cautious without
sticks, spikes or snowshoes. The weather was also getting worse, with clouds
rolling in and reducing visibility.

![The exit of Stuibeneckbahn](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/bolsterlanger_horn_only/2026-01-25-07.jpg)
![The view from Hörnis Nest](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/bolsterlanger_horn_only/2026-01-25-08.jpg)

After a strenuous climb, we finally reached Hörnis Nest, a restaurant near the
summit. The view from here was great, although we had to stay aside from all
ski enthusiasts exiting the ski lift.

![The pretend-cross near the Hörnis Nest restaurant](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/bolsterlanger_horn_only/2026-01-25-09.jpg)
![The view down the slope to Fischen im Allgäu](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/bolsterlanger_horn_only/2026-01-25-10.jpg)

From taking a break at Hörnis Nest, it was a short walk to the summit of
Bolsterlanger Horn (1586 m). The trees on the way up provided some shelter from
the wind and the snow, so we could enjoy the final stretch of the hike walking
on bare ground.

![A sign on the way to the Bolsterlanger Horn (1586 m) summit](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/bolsterlanger_horn_only/2026-01-25-11.jpg)
![Playground near the Hörnis Nest restaurant](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/bolsterlanger_horn_only/2026-01-25-12.jpg)

We reached the summit, took some photos, and enjoyed the panoramic views of the
Allgäu Alps covered in thick clouds. The snowfall was still picking up, so we
decided to head back down after taking another break. We also briefly checked
out the playground on the way back to Hörnis Nest and took a break at the
restaurant itself, but the food prices were a bit steep for my taste.

![Bolsterlanger Horn (1586 m) summit](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/bolsterlanger_horn_only/2026-01-25-13.jpg)
![View from Bolsterlanger Horn (1586 m) onto Wannenkopf (1712 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/bolsterlanger_horn_only/2026-01-25-14.jpg)

The descent was much quicker than the ascent, and we made good time back to
the huts in the middle of the slope. We walked on the side of the ski slope,
sliding down on the icy patches and traversing the fluffy snow otherwise.
The ladies wanted to take yet another break at one of the huts, the
"s'Mibbadin", so I decided to explore the interior in the meantime.

![A piano inside the hut](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/bolsterlanger_horn_only/2026-01-25-15.jpg)

I remembered the Tutzinger Hütte near Benediktenwand (1801 m), which I
[visited in 2024](/hikes/2024-07-21-benediktenwand/). They sold tons of merchandise there, including huts,
keychains, T-shirts, and local drinks and food. Since I was waiting anyway, I
asked around for any souvenirs they may have. Unfortunately, this hut didn't
have anything of the sort, so I headed back to rejoin the ladies. As we exited
the hut and started walking down, an employee I talked with earlier ran out to
hand me a flyer for the mountain, with all the hiking trails and ski slopes
marked on it. That was such a nice gesture! Make sure to check out their
website at [hoernerdoerfer.de](https://www.hoernerdoerfer.de/restaurant-smibbadin-bolsterlang-allgaeu).

![The asphalt road down](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/bolsterlanger_horn_only/2026-01-25-16.jpg)

After saying our goodbyes, we kept going and soon reached the asphalt road
leading back to Bolsterlang. The snow had mostly melted here despite the
surrounding snowfall, making the walk easier. On some icy sections, Natalia used
her sitting pad as a sledge to slide down, while I tripped and fell on my leg,
making me limp for the next few days.

![Snow clearing tractor in Bolsterlang](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/bolsterlanger_horn_only/2026-01-25-17.jpg)

When we reached the village, we realized the one and only bus back to
Fischen im Allgäu would leave in twenty minutes, and we'll have one minute to
change to the train back home. We considered our options, with Kateryna
deciding to stay and the others going by foot. Despite our predictions, we
reached the train station at the same time as the bus with a few minutes to
spare.

To sum up, the hike to Bolsterlanger Horn (1586 m) is a delightful experience
in any season. The well-marked trails, stunning views, and cozy mountain huts
make it accessible and enjoyable for hikers of all levels. Make sure to take
the right gear for the conditions, and don't forget to check out the Hörnerhaus
or the other huts for a refreshing break along the way!

P.S. The surrounding ridges seem very interesting for future hikes, including
the loop back to Fischen im Allgäu and the ridge towards Sonthofen. I'll have
to explore them sometime!
