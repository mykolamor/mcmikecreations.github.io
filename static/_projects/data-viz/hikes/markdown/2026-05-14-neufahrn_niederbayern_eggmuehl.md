The next stop on my way to Regensburg was Eggmühl, the place of the famous
1809 Napoleon Battle. I was a bit sore after playing tennis and table tennis
the day before, so I was planning to do an easier hike. Besides, the forecast
was promising rain throughout the whole day.

![Memorial near the town hall of Neufahrn (Niederbayern)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/neufahrn_niederbayern_eggmuehl/2026-05-14-00.jpg)

The solution was chosen on the fly. I got into the RE 2 train to Hof and sat
there until I heard an announcement for Neufahrn (Niederbayern). I remembered
that I still haven't visited the castle there (which was turned into a hotel),
so it made sense to exit and start hiking from there. As for my destination,
I realized that it takes 30 km to walk from Eggmühl to Saal (Donau), where my
[Kelheim hike ended](/hikes/2025-03-16-riedenburg_kelheim/), so the choice made
sense.

The hike started nicely, with me buying a can of coffee on the train station.
When I was here last time, Neufahrn (Niederbayern) seemed like a small village
with nothing going on. That's because the train station is on the edge of it,
hiding everything interesting out of sight.

![Schlosshotel Neufahrn](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/neufahrn_niederbayern_eggmuehl/2026-05-14-01.jpg)

In reality, they have several churches, Schlosshotel Neufahrn (the castle
hotel), a bunch of sports facilities, their own fire station covering
neighboring villages and more! I didn't linger for too long, since I had a long
way ahead of me, but still took a bit of time to enjoy the scene. There were
also a bunch of ice cream and kebab shops open even on a holiday, but I wasn't
hungry, so I can't give you any reviews.

![Huber Mühle in Oberlindhart](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/neufahrn_niederbayern_eggmuehl/2026-05-14-02.jpg)

After I exited the town, my next stop was Oberlindhart, with their old water
mill turned into a shop. Although the big mill seemed to be made into a
monument there, the smaller mills of Huber Mühle seemed to still be working,
and they offered products for people to buy in their store.

![Stream near the asphalt road to Pfaffenberg](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/neufahrn_niederbayern_eggmuehl/2026-05-14-03.jpg)

From there, I followed the bike road to Pfaffenberg across a few small streams
and fields. The whole way was paved with asphalt, so the walk was quick and
solid. I have to say, the whole route in general and this section in particular
were very nice to go through. The fields of wheat and corn, the occasional
bikers tagging along, everything was great. And the lighting from rainy clouds
gave the whole landscape a painterly feel.

![Horse riders in Pfaffenberg](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/neufahrn_niederbayern_eggmuehl/2026-05-14-04.jpg)

When I entered the town, there was a horse riding competition or exhibition
taking place on my way. It was interesting to check out, although I couldn't
follow what was happening and who was better.

![Entering the Via Nova pilgrimage route at Pfaffenberg](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/neufahrn_niederbayern_eggmuehl/2026-05-14-05.jpg)
![Hunting seat near Holztraubach](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/neufahrn_niederbayern_eggmuehl/2026-05-14-06.jpg)

At this point, my hiking route connected with the Via Nova pilgrimage route.
They had signs at every important turn, benches along the way, some shrines and
small churches to go through. Since this route leads all the way to Eggmühl, I
decided to follow it, making shortcuts when it made sense. This section was
also favored by the local hikers, with many elderly couples taking sections of
the route.

![Pfarrkirche St. Laurentius in Holztraubach](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/neufahrn_niederbayern_eggmuehl/2026-05-14-10.jpg)
![Shortcut from Holztraubach through the woods](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/neufahrn_niederbayern_eggmuehl/2026-05-14-07.jpg)

Just following the Via Nova route, I ended up in Holztraubach. It's a very
small village with a single main road, but I saw the locals celebrating the
holiday with some barbeque near an old tractor they left running. We greeted
each other and I kept going.

![Fields exiting the forest near Holztraubach](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/neufahrn_niederbayern_eggmuehl/2026-05-14-08.jpg)
![Exploring the field flora near Holztraubach](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/neufahrn_niederbayern_eggmuehl/2026-05-14-09.jpg)

The next section was leading me to Pinkofen. Instead of staying on the trail,
which at this point was a nice road paved with stone bricks, I decided to
make a shortcut through the forest. There was a faint track in the dirt
covered in tall grass and shrubs, unmarked on the map (until I fixed it after
getting home). I decided to follow it, finding some dead ends throughout but
eventually getting out of the forest.

![Fields on the hills on the way to Pinkofen](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/neufahrn_niederbayern_eggmuehl/2026-05-14-11.jpg)
![Magic crystal rod from Warcraft on the way to Pinkofen](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/neufahrn_niederbayern_eggmuehl/2026-05-14-12.jpg)

I even saw a deer a few meters away from me. We were both surprised to see each
other, and the deer darted into the woods to get away from me. I also saw some
woodpeckers, but still no foxes, despite being on the lookout for them.

![Exiting the shortcut in the forest near Pinkofen](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/neufahrn_niederbayern_eggmuehl/2026-05-14-13.jpg)
![Wheat fields near Pinkofen](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/neufahrn_niederbayern_eggmuehl/2026-05-14-14.jpg)

I decided to walk past Pinkofen, as it looked like a simple collection of farms
and villas, and headed straight to my final destination. Now that I was back
on the pilgrimage route, I started seeing benches and other hikers again, and
even some locals walking their dogs. Although I saw actual rainfall on the
horizon, there wasn't much wind, so the clouds never moved onto my route. I
managed to stay completely dry through the whole way.

![The Via Nova route after Pinkofen](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/neufahrn_niederbayern_eggmuehl/2026-05-14-15.jpg)
![Blooming trees near Eggmühl](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/neufahrn_niederbayern_eggmuehl/2026-05-14-16.jpg)

When I reached Eggmühl and headed to the train station, I saw Löwendenkmal, a
lion monument built in 1909 to commemorate the 1809 Napoleon Eggmühl battle.
There were information boards and some benches near it for others to find out
more.

![Löwendenkmal Eggmühl](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/neufahrn_niederbayern_eggmuehl/2026-05-14-17.jpg)

All in all, this is a great route and a huge improvement over the Neufahrn
(Niederbayern) to Landshut route. The views are great, the fields are lush,
the forests are great for shade and respite in-between the exposed areas. I
was also really lucky with the weather. I'd highly recommend hikers of all
ages to check out the Via Nova pilgrimage route. You can even get overnight
stay along the route if you want a multi-day trip!
