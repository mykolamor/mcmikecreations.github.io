After I learned that I'll miss all summer solstice celebration events when the
holiday actually happens, I was really disappointed. To my surprise, Lenggries
decided to postpone their event a week due to the heat wave pressing on
Bavaria. They announced that the event will be held on Brauneck (1555 m), which
I conveniently skipped during my [Benediktenwand (1800 m) hike](/hikes/2024-07-21-benediktenwand/).
The danger of fire was still there (and the temperature got even
worse). So the venue made the decision to replace the actual bonfire with a LED
installation, having a fire show with professional showmen instead. With the
[hike to Drachensee](/hikes/2026-06-21-drachensee/)
being on the easier side, this trip also promised to be reasonable, even in
this weather. I sent out the invites to a few hiking chats and my work chat,
but only David from my [Kofel (1342 m) hike](/hikes/2026-05-03-kofel/) responded.

![Nice Lenggries house on the way](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/brauneck_only/2026-06-27-00.jpg)
![Ski lift near the bottom of the slope](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/brauneck_only/2026-06-27-01.jpg)

The route started hot as expected. With 35-36 degrees outside, we just hurried
across the town and the beginner slope to get to the tree shade. The asphalt
was so hot in places that you could see melted bumps and cracks in more exposed
areas of the road. The slope wasn't much better. It's a dirt/gravel road for
offroad vehicles, so every step we took brought a cloud of dust into our lungs.

![Looking down at the dirt road to Brauneck (1555 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/brauneck_only/2026-06-27-02.jpg)
![Looking at Demelspitze (1158 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/brauneck_only/2026-06-27-03.jpg)

It was a bit of a shame to skip a few cliffs and peaks on the way, but the show
at the top should have been worth the sacrifice. Not to get ahead of myself
here, but it definitely was. As we were walking up and chatting, we came to a
group of cows. Normally, we'd just walk around them, but they perked up their
heads and walked over to deliberately block our path. Still, we walked between
them without causing their wrath.

![Cows on the way up](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/brauneck_only/2026-06-27-04.jpg)
![Looking at Speichersee Garland from above](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/brauneck_only/2026-06-27-05.jpg)

As we walked higher and passed lake Speichersee Garland, I started making
breaks more and more often, as the heat started getting to me. This whole
section was again exposed, and the sun was relentless, so it was kind of to be
expected.

![View from Brauneck (1555 m) towards Rechelkopf (1330 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/brauneck_only/2026-06-27-06.jpg)
![View from Brauneck (1555 m) towards Hahn Berg (1321 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/brauneck_only/2026-06-27-07.jpg)

The main nice thing along the way up was looking at the moving cable car and
the mountains across the valley, like Rechelkopf (1330 m) that I
[recently visited](/hikes/2026-05-17-rechelkopf_semmelberg/). The flies were
also becoming an obstacle, some big ones landing on our feet and biting us.
As the preparations for the event were ongoing, SUVs were constantly driving up
and down, tossing up more dust and forcing us onto the bare slope.

![View from Brauneck (1555 m) down to Speichersee Garland](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/brauneck_only/2026-06-27-08.jpg)
![View from Brauneck (1555 m) towards Seekarkreuz (1601 m) and Buchstein (1701 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/brauneck_only/2026-06-27-09.jpg)

As we reached the shallow part of the Brauneck (1555 m) peak, a new panorama
opened up in front of us. To the east, we got to see
[Seekarkreuz (1601 m)](/hikes/2025-03-02-seekarkreuz/) and
[Buchstein (1701 m)](/hikes/2025-10-05-buchstein_only/).
To the west, we saw the continuation of the ridge towards Kochel, including
[Benediktenwand (1800 m)](/hikes/2024-07-21-benediktenwand/).

![View from Brauneck (1555 m) towards Benediktenwand (1800 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/brauneck_only/2026-06-27-10.jpg)
![Paragliders preparing at Brauneck (1555 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/brauneck_only/2026-06-27-11.jpg)

I saw it during my older hikes in the area, but Brauneck (1555 m) is a very
beloved spot for paragliders. The whole sky was covered in them, and they used
the top of the mountain as their starting point. Since we still had more than
two hours until the bonfire event started, we sat on a bench near the
paragliders for a while and observed their preparations. The way they unpacked
the gear, lined up the direction of take-off, untangled and set up the cables,
clipped the straps. Everything was methodical, thought out, calm, almost
meditative. They were mostly silent, everyone paying attention only to
themselves, but activating their walkie-talkies to communicate in the air.
Some of them planned to come down for the event, others just enjoyed the sunset
and flew down to Lenggries.

![The cross at Brauneck (1555 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/brauneck_only/2026-06-27-12.jpg)
![Mykola posing at Brauneck (1555 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/brauneck_only/2026-06-27-13.jpg)

I looked in the direction the paragliders were flying. Quite a few attempted to
glide all the way to Bad Tölz, where I finished my
[Blomberg (1203 m) hike](/hikes/2026-03-15-bichl_blomberg/). In the meantime,
8 o'clock came around, and the sun slowly started to set. The sky, previously
blue and spotless, started gathering clouds and becoming yellow. We took our
chance and went to the restaurant at the cable car building, enjoying some
drinks and a DJ, waiting for the show to get going.

![Paragliders gliding towards Bad Tölz](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/brauneck_only/2026-06-27-14.jpg)
![The start of the sunset on Brauneck (1555 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/brauneck_only/2026-06-27-15.jpg)

As we waited longer, the yellow sun started taking on strawberry-like colors,
with a full pallet of yellows and oranges reflecting off the clouds. It was a
mesmerising view, further enhanced by the relaxed atmosphere of the party.
Everyone around us was enjoying their time, getting cocktails from the bar or
chatting about mundane happenings. Soon after, the organizers lit up the
artificial bonfire, which was already standing out despite the remaining rays
of sun.

![The start of the sunset on Brauneck (1555 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/brauneck_only/2026-06-27-16.jpg)
![The artificial bonfire on Brauneck (1555 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/brauneck_only/2026-06-27-17.jpg)

The sky started turning red, then caught some purple shades and started
dimming. The show was also ready to get started. The hikers and local residents
hurried to the bonfire to get a nice viewing spot. To test the pyrotechnics and
give viewers a test of what's to come, the show started with a nice music piece
with automated fire spewing upwards in tune with the music.

![The first half of the bonfire performance on Brauneck (1555 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/brauneck_only/2026-06-27-19.mp4)
![The artificial bonfire on Brauneck (1555 m) at dusk](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/brauneck_only/2026-06-27-18.jpg)

Then the real display started, with one performer playing an electric guitar
while the other juggled fire rods, crackling ropes, flaming balls and fiery
chains, all while the automatic flamethrowers were still being activated in the
background.

![The second half of the bonfire performance on Brauneck (1555 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/brauneck_only/2026-06-27-20.mp4)

The show ended on a high note, everyone had their fun, thanked the performers
and hurried to the cable car. The last train was leaving in fifty minutes, and
there was still quite a way to go. We took the cable car with a pair of
Lenggries locals, walked over and got to the train with five minutes to spare.
It was definitely one of the more memorable adventures, topping some of my
long-time favorites like [Brünnstein (1619 m)](/hikes/2025-07-06-bruennstein/)
and [Laber (1682 m)](/hikes/2025-04-27-laber_hauptgipfel/).

Now, the conclusion has to be split between the event and the hike. While the
route is easy and accessible for families and pets, the views may not pay off
the required 8 km of distance in each direction. The 14 Euro (as of 2026) cable
car does make it better, but still, [Wallberg (1722 m)](/404/)
may be a better choice for many.

As for the event, I would say it is must-see. Of course, you don't have to go
to Brauneck (1555 m) every year. Feel free to enjoy similar events on other
summits and near mountain huts, or go for a village vibe in the smaller towns
and communities. Still, the unifying atmosphere of a relaxing evening with nice
scenery and a foreground bonfire is similar everywhere, and the occasional
shows around it make the visit even better.
