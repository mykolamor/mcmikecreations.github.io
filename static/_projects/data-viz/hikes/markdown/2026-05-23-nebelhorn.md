As Pavlo, who joined me for the Italian and Austrian hikes, came to visit me in
Munich, I proposed a few possible destinations with picturesque views and
complex routes. They included Ehrwald to Knörrhütte, Karwendel,
[Jenner](/404/),
[Klammspitze](/404/), and
[Nebelhorn](/hikes/2024-10-27-nebelhorn/).
He decided to go with the last option.

![Walking on the road near Oberstdorf](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/nebelhorn/2026-05-23-00.jpg)

We woke up at 4:00 on the day of the hike to a surprise! S1 was under repairs,
and we had to take an extra subway ride to get to the main station. The usual
trains to Oberstdorf were also not working, so we ended up spending 4.5 hours
to get to the town.

We eventually got there, bought some souvenirs, and set off towards Seealpsee.
A big portion of the trail is a largely flat asphalt road with occasional cars
of locals and farm owners passing by. At the end of it, there's a beer garden
and a small museum.

![The trail to Berggasthof Oytalhaus at the bottom of the mountain](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/nebelhorn/2026-05-23-01.jpg)

It was a lovely walk with occasional shade from the trees on the side of the
road. I also got to remember the nearby mountains I went to, namely
[Riefenkopf (1749 m)](/404/) and
[Himmelschrofen (1790 m)](/404/).

The trail here splits into several directions, some leading up and others
deeper in the valley. We took the path to the waterfall, climbed a small
ladder, and started the ascent to the lake.

![Gleitbach waterfall on the way up](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/nebelhorn/2026-05-23-02.jpg)

The route here gets quite difficult from the get go. First comes a slippery
gravel section, then a rocky one. A few times you have to climb over
waterfalls, jumping on rocks sticking out of the water.

![View from the slope towards Riefenkopf (1749 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/nebelhorn/2026-05-23-03.jpg)

This section of the trail is quite barren, covered in shrubs and short grass.
The only views you get are the panorama deeper into the Alps and two mountain
huts on the side of Riefenkopf (1749 m).

![Pavlo cheering himself up to continue the hike](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/nebelhorn/2026-05-23-04.jpg)

As we climbed higher, we started noticing some more spruce trees popping up
here and there. They didn't provide any shade, but it was reassuring that a
less steep section was coming up.

![View deeper into the mountains towards Hornbachjoch (2022 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/nebelhorn/2026-05-23-05.jpg)

And that was indeed the case. At some point, we were facing more tree roots
than rocks on the way, dashing through the open spots to get to the shade
along the trail faster.

![First glimpse at the lake from Mäxeles Egg](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/nebelhorn/2026-05-23-06.jpg)

Soon after, the trees subsided and pine bushes started popping up. We climbed
through a few of them, over some others, across a few more, and got to the
trail overseeing the lake.

After a brief discussion, we headed down to the water surface and enjoyed the
refreshing moist wind near the lake to cool ourselves down from the road so
far. The beach was crowded with tons of tourists, most of them coming from the
top after taking the cable car up.

We stayed a bit longer there, dipping our feet in the water. Soon, something
unusual started happening. The fish in the lake approached me instead of
swimming away, and they even started biting me!

![Enjoying Seealpsee with a drink](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/nebelhorn/2026-05-23-16.jpg)

It tickled at first, and I didn't feel any harm. Still, they started biting
harder and harder, making it more and more painful. At last, I gave up and
took my feet out of the water. Meanwhile, some butterflies sat on me and
weren't even trying to get off.

![View from the Hintere Seealpe hut](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/nebelhorn/2026-05-23-07.jpg)
![View from Zeigersattel (1922 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/nebelhorn/2026-05-23-08.jpg)

We had some drinks and food and headed up. Id say the route up to the lake is
the most challenging and demanding part of the hike, and the trail onwards gets
better. It's not as steep and the road itself is wider.

![Nebelhorn (2224 m) slope](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/nebelhorn/2026-05-23-09.jpg)
![View from Nebelhorn (2224 m) back onto the ridge](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/nebelhorn/2026-05-23-10.jpg)

We kept going for a while, walking alongside some tourist groups having
snowball fights on the trail. I even posed as a local guide, showing some older
hikers the way to the lake and down the mountain.

![An eagle on Nebelhorn (2224 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/nebelhorn/2026-05-23-11.jpg)
![View from Nebelhorn (2224 m) towards Western Wengenkopf (2235 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/nebelhorn/2026-05-23-12.jpg)

When we got to the saddle leading to the second to last station, Pavlo started
feeling bad. We took another break, thought about it, and decided to take the
cable car instead. Once we got over the hurdle of paying the elderly lady at
the ticket register, we took the tickets and headed up to the summit of
Nebelhorn (2224 m).

![View from Nebelhorn (2224 m) towards Entschenkopf (2043 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/nebelhorn/2026-05-23-13.jpg)
![Mykola and Pavlo on Nebelhorn (2224 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/nebelhorn/2026-05-23-14.jpg)

There were tons of tourists there, a lady with an eagle on a leash, several
Alpine Choughts that I fed with some crumbs, and a few larger groups of hikers
enjoying the views. Not that we weren't in a hurry, we explored the
surroundings a bit, got some food and drinks, and enjoyed the rest. We came
down by cable car too.

![Celebrating the climb onto Nebelhorn (2224 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/nebelhorn/2026-05-23-15.jpg)

If you ever decide to visit there, which I would highly recommend, I would take
the cable car up and either go down to the lake, or explore the neighboring
peaks and ridges. Nebelhorn (2224 m) has a lot to offer but somehow the station
itself wasn't as interesting as the rest.
