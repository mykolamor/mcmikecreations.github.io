When my Ukrainian friend Serhii, who lives in Lisbon, approached me for an easy
hike near Munich, I decided to offer a peak I haven't done before. Out of the
easily accessible ones, the area between Tegernsee and Spitzingsee seemed to
offer a good option. Since I've already done hikes to [Wasserspitz (1552 m)](/hikes/2025-09-07-wasserspitz/),
and [Bodenschneid (1669 m)](/hikes/2025-11-01-bodenschneid/),
Rainerkopf (1463 m) was the only remaining
peak on that ridge. Don't confuse it with [Rainer Berg (1169 m)](/hikes/2025-10-27-auer_berg/),
which is a different mountain near Spitzingsee.

![Frozen bushes in Neuhaus](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/rainerkopf/2025-12-23-00.jpg)

We set of in the cold foggy valley of Schliersee, freezing along the way, just
as the surrounding flora. The fog lasted for around 30 minutes, until we reached
a higher altitude.

![Bittersweet nightshade on the way up](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/rainerkopf/2025-12-23-02.jpg)

As we walked higher, we had to take off our jackets, as the sun started to
shine through the clouds. We came across a lady looking for shortcuts down to
Neuhaus, probably late for the train.

![Hikers staring at Serhii](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/rainerkopf/2025-12-23-01.jpg)

When we came up to a hut on the way, Serhii started feeling really hot and
decided to take of his thermal underpants. Since there was no dresser around,
he just dropped them on the ground and started changing. A group of hikers
approached us and started a small chat right near us, standing and throwing
glances at Serhii. It made for a great situation.

![View back at Wasserspitz (1552 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/rainerkopf/2025-12-23-03.jpg)
![View towards Wendelstein (1838 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/rainerkopf/2025-12-23-04.jpg)

We quickly ascended the hill to reach Rainerkopf (1463 m) itself, where we
saw the fog below and took a bunch of photos. There was also a small cross
and a casket with a book to sign in, which we promptly did. Despite the fog,
we managed to enjoy views of surrounding peaks, including [Wendelstein (1838 m)](/hikes/2025-06-10-wendelstein/).

![Rainerkopf (1463 m) cross](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/rainerkopf/2025-12-23-05.jpg)

The descent to Bodenschneidhaus (1365 m) was quick and easy, but due to time
constraints, we decided to skip it and head forward, going past [Brecherspitz (1685 m)](/hikes/2025-08-31-brecherspitz/).

![Towards Bodenschneidhaus (1365 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/rainerkopf/2025-12-23-06.jpg)
![View of Brecherspitz (1685 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/rainerkopf/2025-12-23-07.jpg)

That's when the route turned to the worse. The trail to Obere Firstalm (1369 m)
was very slippery due to ice, and we had to be very careful with each step.
We kept slipping and sliding, and Serhii hurt his leg a few times.

![Icy road towards Obere Firstalm (1369 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/rainerkopf/2025-12-23-08.jpg)
![View towards Stümpfling (1506 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/rainerkopf/2025-12-23-10.jpg)

Eventually, we made it to the hut, where we took a break and had some food,
enjoying the view of [Stümpfling (1506 m)](/hikes/2025-09-07-wasserspitz/).
I really wanted their permutation of Jagatee, and took some cake with it.

![Waiting for Jagatee](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/rainerkopf/2025-12-23-09.jpg)

After spending some time at the hut, we headed down the sledge route down,
although there was no snow for sledging. When we reached the asphalt road
at the bottom, we saw the fog flowing over the ridge and towards Spitzingsee.
The lake itself was almost completely frozen, with people of all ages skating
and walking on it. We walked on the ice along the lake shore to the bus stop
and headed back.

![Foggy icy Spitzingsee](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/rainerkopf/2025-12-23-11.jpg)

I wouldn't say this is the most breathtaking hike in the area, I'd definitely
suggest [Bodenschneid (1669 m)](/hikes/2025-11-01-bodenschneid/) instead, but it's a good option if
you've seen the other peaks already.
