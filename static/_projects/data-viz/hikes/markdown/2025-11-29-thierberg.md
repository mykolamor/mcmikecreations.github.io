The main and only goal of this hike organized by Annette from DAAD FK was to
have a chill walk to Kufstein from a nearby train stop. After I fractured my
finger a week ago on the [last hike to Vordere Kesselschneid (2002 m)](/hikes/2025-11-23-vordere_kesselschneid/)
I was limited in the gear I could wear or use, so joining was a perfect way to
get some fresh air without overexerting myself.

![Frozen nature](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/thierberg/2025-11-29-00.jpg)

The journey began as usual, with someone from the group missing the train. We
arrived at the station and started going on the Kieferer Energiewanderweg.

![Electricity generator in Kiefersfelden](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/thierberg/2025-11-29-01.jpg)

Despite the snow on the mountain tops, the path down below was fully bare,
allowing everyone to walk safely.

![Waterfall along the way](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/thierberg/2025-11-29-02.jpg)

After we passed a nice waterfall and took some group pictures, we headed to the
nearby lake and enjoyed the view of the distant mountains together with some
ducks and large fish. Along the way we had to take a few detours due to closed
off roads, but nothing major. I got to enjoy [Pyramidenspitze (1997 m)](/hikes/2025-02-02-pyramidenspitze/)
in the distance from there as well.

![Hechtsee with ducks](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/thierberg/2025-11-29-03.jpg)
![View towards Pyramidenspitze (1997 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/thierberg/2025-11-29-04.jpg)
![View towards Reinhardsberg (1400 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/thierberg/2025-11-29-05.jpg)

Then, we headed down to Thierberg (721 m) to the small chapel they have there.
Although there was construction work happening there, we were allowed to climb
the stairs to the top of the tower, opening a great view of the surrounding
mountains and Kufstein itself.

![Setting up the Christmas tree](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/thierberg/2025-11-29-12.jpg)
![View from Burgruine Thierberg towards Pyramidenspitze (1997 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/thierberg/2025-11-29-06.jpg)
![View from Burgruine Thierberg onto Wörgl](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/thierberg/2025-11-29-07.jpg)

We also realized that the construction was to put and light up a Christmas tree
on the top of the tower. We watched the tree being brought to the top and took
some more photos, then headed down to the city.

![Pfrillsee](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/thierberg/2025-11-29-08.jpg)
![Kufstein train station](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/thierberg/2025-11-29-09.jpg)

After another small walk near a lake on the way, we reached the downtown area
and headed to the fortress, where we enjoyed even more view during dawn and
headed straight to the Christmas market.

![View from Festung Kufstein](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/thierberg/2025-11-29-10.jpg)

For beginners not experienced in hiking up mountains, this is a good route
offering nice views as a distraction from the city life. Have fun!

![Christmas market inside Festung Kufstein](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/thierberg/2025-11-29-11.jpg)

P.S. I couldn't wear gloves because of the cast for the fractured finger, so my
hands were freezing the whole time.
