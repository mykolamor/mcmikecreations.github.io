On my last visit to [Nebelhorn (2224 m) with Pavlo](/hikes/2026-05-23-nebelhorn/)
I promised him I would visit Sviatoslav and Pavlo back in Poland at some point by
the end of the year. Well, a few months later, I finally found time to commit
to a trip there. Since I was interested in hiking and Warsaw didn't offer much
for it, we decided to travel to Zakopane and explore hikes in that area instead.

Although the weather report for the dates of the trip looked okay, it had been snowing
continuously for several days prior. We made a plan: finish an easy hike on the
first full day of the vacation, scout the area, get a feel for the weather, then
do a big-boy hike on the second day. Thus, we chose the most popular and easily
accessible peak, Kasprowy Wierch (1987 m).

We didn't really have a plan going in. After taking the cable car, we stepped
out onto the viewing area and immediately got hit by a freezing wind inside a
cloud surrounding the mountains. After a little consideration, we opted for the
trail along the ridge to Kopa Kondracka (2005 m), given the trail was clear and
the weather got better.

![View from the cable car going towards Kasprowy Wierch (1987 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/kasprowy_wierch/2026-11-14-00.jpg)
![Starting the descent from Kasprowy Wierch (1987 m) towards Kopa Kondracka (2005 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/kasprowy_wierch/2026-11-14-01.jpg)

When we got to the top of Kasprowy Wierch (1987 m) and didn't see anything, the
first thing we noticed was the even more freezing wind blowing in our faces and
hands. We quickly put on shoe spikes, gaiters, mittens, and other available gear
to combat the frost. From there, we started our way along the ridge, hoping for
the clouds to move away from the mountain.

![View from the ridge towards Goryczkowa Czuba (1912 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/kasprowy_wierch/2026-11-14-02.jpg)
![View of the cliff of Pośrednia Sucha Czuba Kondracka (1844 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/kasprowy_wierch/2026-11-14-03.jpg)

The start of the trail was nice. Many hikers were attempting the same route
ahead of us, clearing a comfortable track through the snow for us to walk on.
Although we came across a few slippery sections, we kept going and soon after
reached Pośredni Goryczkowy Wierch (1874 m). It was my first time on this ridge,
so I was a bit worried we'd have to climb these cliffs under harsh weather
conditions. Pavlo, who had visited this place before, assured me that there was a
trail going through the slope under the ridge.

![View from Pośrednia Sucha Czuba Kondracka (1844 m) southwards](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/kasprowy_wierch/2026-11-14-04.jpg)
![A crossroads sign on the way to Kopa Kondracka (2005 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/kasprowy_wierch/2026-11-14-05.jpg)

The more we kept walking forward, the more hikers we started to pass. On the one
hand, these hikers were quite slow despite the decent trail we were taking. It
felt natural, when the opportunity presented itself, to walk past them. On the other
hand, having fewer people ahead of us meant potentially having to dig through the
snow ourselves, like I did on my dangerous [Sonnenberg (1622 m) trip](/hikes/2026-02-15-oberammergau_sonnenberg/).

![One of the final hills towards Kopa Kondracka (2005 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/kasprowy_wierch/2026-11-14-06.jpg)
![Pavlo at the top of Kopa Kondracka (2005 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/kasprowy_wierch/2026-11-14-07.jpg)

When we reached the saddle going towards Kopa Kondracka (2005 m), we came across
several small groups of tourists and hikers going in the opposite direction,
towards the cable car station. Again, although this meant more crowds on the
summit, it also meant the path ahead of us was clear and well-explored. We
kept going past a group of mountain climbers changing equipment on the saddle,
and found ourselves at our destination soon after.

![A peak northwards over the ridge near Suchá kopa (1895 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/kasprowy_wierch/2026-11-14-08.jpg)
![View from the Suchá kopa (1895 m) ridge towards Kasprowy Wierch (1987 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/kasprowy_wierch/2026-11-14-09.jpg)

The peak was nothing to write home about. A wide flat area with a boundary stone
denoting the border and a signpost giving directions to nearby summits. I wasn't
too thrilled about being blown away by the wind and fog either. Sviatoslav
suggested we could take a break, have some food and enjoy the summit. Any other
day I would have agreed, but the wind covering my eyes with sharp chunks of snow
and ice didn't let me have second thoughts.

![Up-close view of Goryczkowa Czuba (1912 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/kasprowy_wierch/2026-11-14-10.jpg)
![A small climb near Pośrednia Sucha Czuba Kondracka (1844 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/kasprowy_wierch/2026-11-14-11.jpg)

On the way back, we came across quite a few more groups of hikers going in both
directions. I was surprised how over-prepared some of them were, carrying ice
pickaxes, big glacier spikes, helmets, rarely ropes. The trail was perfectly
accessible to people of all ages (although there's quite a bit of total
elevation gain across the hills, cliffs, and summits), so all that equipment was
a bit unwarranted.

![View from Pośrednia Sucha Czuba Kondracka (1844 m) across the border towards Tichá dolina](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/kasprowy_wierch/2026-11-14-12.jpg)
![The hole-covered cliff of Pośrednia Sucha Czuba Kondracka (1844 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/kasprowy_wierch/2026-11-14-13.jpg)

On the way back, Sviatoslav's condition started to decline. Him not being
used to mountain hiking, the harsh windy conditions, the uncertainty of the
route duration and distance, and the fact that the initial plan for an easy
scouting hike had turned into a full-blown mountain adventure made him go
slower and slower, coughing and taking breaks.

![Ridge trail towards Wysoka Sucha Czuba Kondracka (1876 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/kasprowy_wierch/2026-11-14-14.jpg)
![Goryczkowa Czuba (1912 m) up close](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/kasprowy_wierch/2026-11-14-15.jpg)

As we walked closer to the cable car station and finally saw it over the
neighboring peaks, we had to figure out what to do with Sviatoslav. He wasn't
feeling good and needed a good break, but the weather just didn't allow for it,
although the clouds dispersed a bit. We made a few longer breaks to allow him to
recover a little bit, but ultimately decided to interrupt the hike back at the
cable car station.

![View from Goryczkowa Czuba (1912 m) towards the source of the Zadná Tichá stream](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/kasprowy_wierch/2026-11-14-16.jpg)
![Sviatoslav taking a break on the border stone near Kasprowy Wierch (1987 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/kasprowy_wierch/2026-11-14-17.jpg)

I was really interested to visit another summit since we were in the area and
potentially had to end the hike abruptly, so I talked to the others and we made
the decision to split up. Sviatoslav would finish the leftover bit of the route
towards the restaurant in the cable car station, while Pavlo would join me and
visit Beskyd (2012 m).

![View from the border towards Tichý vrch (1979 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/kasprowy_wierch/2026-11-14-18.jpg)
![View over the saddle from Kasprowy Wierch (1987 m) towards Świnica (2302 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/kasprowy_wierch/2026-11-14-19.jpg)

While Sviatoslav was sitting on a border stone catching his breath, I urged
Pavlo to skip going all the way up to Kasprowy Wierch (1987 m) and instead take
a detour around the mountain. Logically speaking, the slope was quite shallow,
so we should have been able to wrap around the summit and hop onto the trail
further along, saving some time and effort.

![View from Beskyd (2012 m) towards Kasprowy Wierch (1987 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/kasprowy_wierch/2026-11-14-20.jpg)
![View from Kasprowy Wierch (1987 m) towards Kriváň (2494 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/kasprowy_wierch/2026-11-14-21.jpg)

Soon after I suggested the idea to Pavlo, we came across some footprints in the
ankle-deep snow wrapping around the mountain and followed them. Although this
temporary trail was improvised, we managed to follow it all the way through,
finish the shortcut and walk our way up to Beskyd (2012 m). The way going there
was very easy, although covered in ice a bit. All the tourists taking the cable
car headed there, and we had to navigate around groups taking photos, people
carrying long selfie sticks, and hardcore mountain climbers in full gear
descending from Świnica (2302 m).

We came back to the restaurant, found Sviatoslav enjoying a meal and feeling
much better, had a meal of our own, and started discussing further plans. Our
original idea for day 2 was taking the Zawrat (2159 m) mountain pass from the
lakes to the south near Świnica (2302 m) northwards to Kuźnice. With the snow
around and the cold weather it was clear that we were overworked and
underprepared for a hike of this caliber. We settled on going down on foot, visit
some lakes on the way, and walk down to the village.

![Looking at Litworowy Staw Gąsienicowy (1618 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/kasprowy_wierch/2026-11-14-22.jpg)
![The trail to Zielony Staw Gąsienicowy (1672 m) with a view towards Pośrednia Turnia (2128 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/kasprowy_wierch/2026-11-14-23.jpg)

We equipped our gaiters and spikes and started our descent. From the big crowds
following the trail down I assumed the way there was very wide and accessible,
potentially even accessible to off-road vehicles. I was completely wrong.
Although many parts of the trail were wide enough for a few people to walk
side-by-side, it was still very much a footpath. We took it all the way down,
then turned around and took a smaller trail towards
Zielony Staw Gąsienicowy (1672 m).

![Standing at the edge of Zielony Staw Gąsienicowy (1672 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/kasprowy_wierch/2026-11-14-24.jpg)
![Sunset at Litworowy Staw Gąsienicowy (1618 m) from above](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/kasprowy_wierch/2026-11-14-25.jpg)

It was getting dark, but we still met occasional hikers going to and from the
lake. At one point, we had to jump over a small stream flowing between the
numerous lakes and puddles in the area. The small pines, the evening lights,
the chill quiet atmosphere often interrupted by Sviatoslav's complaining about
camera quality, iPhone inferiority, and his financial decisions. What a trip to
remember!

![Standing at Przełęcz między Kopami (1499 m) overlooking Zakopane](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/kasprowy_wierch/2026-11-14-26.jpg)
![Walking through Kuźnice](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/kasprowy_wierch/2026-11-14-27.jpg)

From the lakes, we walked down past some houses, mountain huts, and lodges;
trekked along and around many other small groups; slipped through the
ice-covered forest and shallow slopes. Eventually, we ended up in Kuźnice with
all the restaurants and shops already closed, and took the bus back to our
accommodation.

To summarize, I would definitely recommend this route as a whole or parts of it
to any fit hiker of any age. I would not, however, recommend it in winter. For
fit hikers, the cloud-covered summits are just not worth the commute. For unfit
hikers, the snow and ice add a significant challenge. In summer, the trail gets
very crowded, making you lose a lot of time waiting in lines to pass through
narrow sections of the ridge. Make sure to check the conditions before going
there!
