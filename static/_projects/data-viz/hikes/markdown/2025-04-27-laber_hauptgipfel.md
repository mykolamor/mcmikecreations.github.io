I organized this hike with the DAAD scholars and David as a way to ease more
people into hiking, as many participants from before have finished their
studies and left Germany. We started off pretty rough with getting stuck on the
way because of a tree that had fallen on the road. Luckily, the fire brigade
was already there to clean up the mess, so we could continue our way. Still,
David got confused, left the bus one stop earlier and had to catch up to us.

![Foggy forest on the way up](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/laber_hauptgipfel/2025-04-27-00.jpg)

As we started walking up the hill, we were greeted with the fog and/or clouds
covering the mountain. It was a bit chilly in some places, hot in others, but
nothing too distracting. Eventually we climbed out of the fog and were greeted
with a beautiful view of the mountains and the valley below in the direction
of Garmisch.

![Veiw from the slope onto Notkarspitze (1888 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/laber_hauptgipfel/2025-04-27-01.jpg)

We kept going, making breaks to take pictures and let other hikers through
until we reached the first summit, Schartenkopf (1636 m). There we made
a longer lunch break in case the Alpine hut was closed. It was very sunny
and the view above the clouds was breathtaking. We could see the [Zugspitze (2962 m)](/hikes/2025-06-21-zugspitze_reintal/) and
the Alps in the distance. We took a lot of pictures and enjoyed the view.

![Veiw from Schartenkopf (1636 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/laber_hauptgipfel/2025-04-27-02.jpg)

After a while, we kept going to Laber Hauptgipfel (1686 m), which apparently
didn't have a cross despite being the main summit. Nobody was disappointed
though, because we were on the way to a beer garden which may have been open.
To our amusement, the beer garden was indeed open, so we had a drink there too.
I also tried the main attraction of the place, the Alpine ice cream, which
was delicious and I recommend it to everyone. As we were sitting there,
the rising clouds finally caught up to us, and we were surrounded by fog again.

![Way down from Laber (1682 m)](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/laber_hauptgipfel/2025-04-27-03.jpg)

We took the way down to the valley, covered with many flowers, including
Soldanellas. As we left the mountain, the exciting part of the hike finished
and the rest of the way was just a walk down and through the city towards
the train station.

![Soldanella on the Laber (1682 m) mountainside](/images/projects/data-viz/hikes/stories/laber_hauptgipfel/2025-04-27-04.jpg)

In conclusion, it was an easy and nice hike, perfect for sporty beginners. The
route is pretty safe for most of the year and some steeper parts are secured
with a metal rope for those who need it. The view towards the [Zugspitze (2962 m)](/hikes/2025-06-21-zugspitze_reintal/) and
the Alps is great and the ice cream in the beer garden is a must-try.
